Breach -- Kessler on the SOMA skeleton (game file for the pose layer)
=====================================================================

FILES
  soma_kessler.glb   one skinned mesh (albedo + normal embedded, gloss in the albedo's alpha),
                     the 30-joint SOMA skeleton, 15 animation clips. Body only: the carbine and
                     any other prop attach to a bone in the pose layer.

MESH AND TEXTURES
  Kessler's game mesh from branch 33-kessler (art/characters/kessler/, see its LICENSE and
  PROVENANCE.md): an AI image-to-3D model Erik Steen generated in Krea. Commercial-use terms
  follow his Krea plan -- TO BE CONFIRMED by Erik before any release.

SKELETON AND ANIMATIONS
  Generated with NVIDIA Kimodo (model Kimodo-SOMA-RP-v1.2, https://github.com/nv-tlabs/kimodo) on the
  SOMA skeleton, under the NVIDIA Open Model License; see
  docs/animation_milestones_design_2026-11-02.md section 8. Weights come from Kimodo's skinned SOMA
  body. Built by local-models: blender/retarget_soma.py, tools/pack_clips.py, tools/soma30.py.
