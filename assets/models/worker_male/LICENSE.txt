Breach -- male worker game model
===================================

FILES
  worker_male.glb   rigged, textured male worker: one skinned mesh (albedo + normal map embedded),
                     the 53-bone humanoid skeleton, 46 animation clips
  worker_male_white_clean_albedo.png   (where present) the albedo of the white_clean look variant,
                     baked onto the same mesh and UVs as the .glb's own texture

MESH AND TEXTURES
  Generated by the scripts in art/characters/ of this repository (the character's tables in
  art/characters/worker_male/scripts/worker.py, the shared kit in art/characters/charkit/):
  procedural geometry, voxel-remeshed and decimated, textures baked from the scripted
  high-resolution model. No third-party geometry or textures.
  Rebuild:  cd art/characters/worker_male
            blender -b --factory-startup -P scripts/game.py                         (everything, ~3.5 min)
            blender -b --factory-startup -P scripts/game.py -- --rig-only           (rig stage only)
            blender -b --factory-startup -P scripts/game.py -- --variant white_clean (a look variant's albedo)
  Method and measurements: art/characters/worker_male/PROVENANCE.md.

SKELETON AND ANIMATIONS
  Quaternius -- "Universal Animation Library" (https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0.
  The skeleton's names and hierarchy and the 46 clips come from
  assets/models/marine/AnimationLibrary_Godot_Standard.gltf; they were re-proportioned to
  this character and re-baked onto its own rest pose (arms down) by
  art/characters/charkit/rig.py, the feet kept out of the floor by its floor clamp.
  See assets/models/marine/LICENSE.txt and assets/models/marine/LICENSE_quaternius.txt for
  the source and the full CC0 text.
