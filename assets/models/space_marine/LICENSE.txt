Breach -- space marine game model
=================================

FILES
  space_marine.glb   rigged, textured space marine: one skinned mesh (albedo + normal map
                     embedded), the 53-bone humanoid skeleton, 46 animation clips

MESH AND TEXTURES
  Generated by the scripts in art/characters/ of this repository (the character in
  art/characters/space_marine_claude/, the shared kit in art/characters/charkit/):
  procedural geometry, voxel-remeshed and decimated, textures baked from the scripted
  high-resolution model. No third-party geometry or textures.
  Rebuild:  cd art/characters/space_marine_claude
            blender -b --factory-startup -P scripts/game.py            (everything, ~2 min)
            blender -b --factory-startup -P scripts/game.py -- --rig-only   (rig stage only)
  Method and measurements: art/characters/space_marine_claude/PROVENANCE.md.

SKELETON AND ANIMATIONS
  Quaternius -- "Universal Animation Library" (https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0.
  The skeleton's names and hierarchy and the 46 clips come from
  assets/models/marine/AnimationLibrary_Godot_Standard.gltf; they were re-proportioned to
  this character and re-baked onto its own rest pose (arms down) by
  art/characters/charkit/rig.py. See assets/models/marine/LICENSE.txt and
  assets/models/marine/LICENSE_quaternius.txt for the source and the full CC0 text.
