Breach -- The space marine on the SOMA skeleton (game file for the pose layer)
=====================================================================

FILES
  soma_space_marine.glb one skinned mesh (albedo + normal embedded, gloss in the albedo's alpha),
                     the 30-joint SOMA skeleton, 27 animation clips. Body only: the carbine and
                     any other prop attach to a bone in the pose layer.

MESH AND TEXTURES
  The mesh and textures of assets/models/space_marine/space_marine.glb: procedural geometry made by
  this repository's scripts, no third-party geometry or textures. See assets/models/space_marine/LICENSE.txt.

SKELETON AND ANIMATIONS
  Generated with NVIDIA Kimodo (model Kimodo-SOMA-RP-v1.2, https://github.com/nv-tlabs/kimodo) on the
  SOMA skeleton, under the NVIDIA Open Model License; see
  docs/animation_milestones_design_2026-11-02.md section 8. Weights come from Kimodo's skinned SOMA
  body. Built by local-models: blender/retarget_soma.py, tools/pack_clips.py, tools/soma30.py
  (the whole file: motion/pack_marine_v2.sh).
