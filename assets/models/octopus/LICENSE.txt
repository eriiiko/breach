Breach -- octopus game model (the creature look, #106 P7)
=========================================================

FILES
  octopus.glb   rigged, textured octopus at the "mid" size (body 1.4 m, arm 2.52 m at
                rest): one skinned mesh (12,200 triangles; albedo with gloss in ALPHA
                and a tangent normal map embedded), 142 bones (6 body + 8 arms x 17
                joints, octopus_math.bones("game")), one static "Idle_Loop" clip (a
                loader token only: the game poses every bone from the simulated arm
                chain, renderer/creature_pose.py)

PROVENANCE
  A BYTE-FOR-BYTE copy (packaged 2026-10-31, #106 P7) of
  art/characters/octopus/prototype/octopus/octopus_game.glb at commit 76ffbb2
  (2026-10-28, "octopus rebuilt at the new default mid"),
  sha256 bec6bc764ae7eec4bc63f8a5f925e59bdccce70ca51480541dc1ebc5e0cc4900.

  Generated by the scripts in art/characters/octopus/scripts/ of this repository:
    octopus_math.py   the body, crown/web junction, arm geometry, rig, skin weights
                      and the SIZES table (pure numpy)
    arm_math.py       the one-arm proof it builds on (taper, quaternion)
    build_octopus.py  the Blender build: hero mesh, the selected-to-active bake
                      (diffuse, tangent normal, roughness -> gloss in alpha) and the
                      game GLB export (glbtex.replace_image splices the albedo)
  Procedural geometry and textures, made in this repository; no third-party
  geometry or textures. Rebuild and method: art/characters/octopus/README.md.

  The renderer refuses this file if its bind pose is not the rig's bind pose at
  the size the [creatures.octopus] row names (renderer/creature_model.py
  check_bind_pose, 1 mm): a rebuild at another size must be copied here again.
