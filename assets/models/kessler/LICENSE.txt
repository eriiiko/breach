Breach -- Kessler game model
============================

FILES
  kessler.glb   rigged, textured EVA-suit marine "Kessler": one skinned mesh (albedo with the
                gloss mask in its alpha + normal map, embedded), the 53-bone humanoid skeleton,
                46 animation clips

MESH AND TEXTURES
  Source: an AI image-to-3D model generated by Erik Steen in Krea (krea.ai) from a concept sheet
  he also generated there (2026-10-25/26): art/characters/kessler/source/kessler_source.glb and
  art/characters/kessler/reference/. Licence of the source: AI-generated on Erik's Krea account;
  commercial-use terms follow his Krea plan -- TO BE CONFIRMED by Erik before any release.
  This file was made from it by the scripts of this repository: the visor replaced by a smooth
  glass cap, voxel-remeshed and decimated to one skin, textures baked from the source's own
  maps (art/characters/kessler/scripts/game.py, the shared kit in art/characters/charkit/).
  Rebuild:  blender -b --factory-startup -P art/characters/kessler/scripts/game.py
            blender -b --factory-startup -P art/characters/kessler/scripts/game.py -- --rig-only
  Provenance and decisions: art/characters/kessler/PROVENANCE.md.

SKELETON AND ANIMATIONS
  Quaternius -- "Universal Animation Library" (https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0.
  The skeleton's names and hierarchy and the 46 clips come from
  assets/models/marine/AnimationLibrary_Godot_Standard.gltf; they were re-proportioned to
  this character and re-baked onto its own rest pose (his T-pose) by
  art/characters/charkit/rig.py. See assets/models/marine/LICENSE.txt and
  assets/models/marine/LICENSE_quaternius.txt for the source and the full CC0 text.
