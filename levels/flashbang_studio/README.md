# Flashbang studio

The blast flash's look studio (G4 P5, #75). Two identical sealed rooms, each with a
`flashbang` timed charge at its centre, firing at 2 s (tick 48, the same tick as
`levels/blast_final`'s volley) and then every 8 s. The east room has a cold soot
column (`[payloads.studio_smoke]`, no blast, no heat) laid 1.5 s before the flash,
4 tiles east of the charge, so the column absorbs the flash and casts its shadow.
The lamps are the blast studios' (9 per room at 0.4).

GENERATED by `tools/gen_flashbang_studio.py` through `level_lib`; edit the
constants there and re-run. The flash dials are `[payloads.flashbang] flash` and
`flash_decay_s` in `config.toml` (PLACEHOLDERS, Erik tunes).
