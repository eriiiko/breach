# Smoke-light studio

The still-picture level for tuning smoke against light (#12, fire + smoke
session). It uses the explosion studio's rooms, a light grey floor and nine
lights. Generated by `tools/gen_smoke_light_studio.py` through `level_lib`.

## Launch (a frozen frame)

    C:/Users/steen/anaconda3/python.exe main.py --level smoke_light_studio --control gamepad --warp 3.0

`--warp 3.0` runs the sim 3.0 s on the sim clock before the first frame, then
pauses it. Under `--control gamepad` there is no pause key, so the frame stays
frozen; the camera, zoom, F4 (light), F6 (tile inspector) and T (temperature)
still work. Under `--control wego` (the default) Space resumes, and the sim
pauses again at the 10 s round seam.

**The same `--warp` always gives the same picture.** The sim is
deterministic, so no snapshot file is needed. To tune:

1. Edit a dial in `config.toml`.
2. Quit and launch again with the same `--warp`.
3. Compare.

The render and light dials (`[render.gas_medium]`, `[render.lighting]`,
`[smoke]`, the smoke row's `absorption` / `scatter_albedo`) never feed the
sim, so the smoke lies exactly where it did. Payload rows and
`[physics.*]` dials DO move the sim, so they change the smoke itself.

Other moments: `--warp 2.5` is the densest (the big cloud 0.5 s old);
`--warp 3.5` to `4.5` gives thinner, smoother smoke.

## What is in it

Three charges fire ONCE, half a second apart:

| Charge | Room | Payload | At |
|---|---|---|---|
| `charge_small` | NE | `studio_small` | 1.0 s |
| `charge_medium` | SE | `studio_medium` | 1.5 s |
| `charge_large` | west hall | `studio_large` | 2.0 s |

The crates catch fire and keep glowing.

The lights are placed against the smoke as it lies at `--warp 3.0`:

- **Four corner lamps** (the explosion studio's) sit in clean air, as the
  reference.
- **West hall, spot from the west wall, aimed east (cool white).** The beam
  runs from clear air into the dense cloud piled on the east wall.
- **West hall, dim lamp inside that cloud (warm).** Light from within the
  smoke.
- **NE corner spot (warm) and SE corner spot (cool).** Each beam crosses its
  room's cloud: one warm, one cool beam through similar smoke.
- **NE room, spot aimed west through the doorway (warm).** It backlights the
  plume that pours through the door.

## Editing

The lights are `[[light]]` rows in `level.toml`. You can edit them there, or
edit `LIGHTS` in the generator and re-run it; the generator rewrites
`level.toml`, `tilemap.csv` and `diffuse.png`, but not this README.

A spot is a fixed cone:

    kind = "spot"
    beam_deg = 25.0
    phase = 0.375

`phase` is the aim, as a fraction of a turn: 0 east, 0.25 south, 0.5 west,
0.75 north. `beam_deg` is the cone's full width.
