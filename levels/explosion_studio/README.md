# Explosion studio

The test level for the #31 "explosion + air" session
(`docs/prep_patches_handoff_2026-09-30.md` P5). Generated by
`tools/gen_explosion_studio.py` through `level_lib`; the charges are
`timed_charge` entities (`[[entity]]` rows in `level.toml`) and their yields
are the provisional `[payloads.studio_*]` rows in `config.toml`.

## Launch (continuous time)

    C:/Users/steen/anaconda3/python.exe main.py --level explosion_studio --control gamepad

`--control gamepad` picks the `ContinuousRealtime` ruleset: the sim starts
unpaused and runs with no rounds, phases or auto-pause. Camera, zoom and the
renderer keys (F4 light, F6 tile inspector, ...) work from keyboard and mouse.
Without a controller nothing drives a unit, which this level does not need (it
has no spawns). There is no pause key under this scheme.

Fallback (the accepted gap): `--control wego` (the default), press Space to
unpause; the WEGO round seam pauses the sim every 10 s, press Space again. The
charges run on the sim clock either way.

## What happens

48 x 48 hull at 1 m tiles inside a 4-tile vacuum band (`boundary = "space"`),
three rooms joined by open doorways, furniture crates (fuel) beside the
charges. Every charge repeats every 5 s after its first detonation.

| Charge | Where | Payload | First |
|---|---|---|---|
| `charge_small` | NE room | `studio_small` (frag_heat x1, r 4) | 2 s |
| `charge_medium` | SE room | `studio_medium` (frag_heat x2, r 5) | 4 s |
| `charge_large` | west hall | `studio_large` (frag_heat x4, r 6) | 6 s |
| `charge_hull` | NE room, north hull | `studio_hull` (wall_damage 800 + heat) | 8 s |

The hull charge blows a ~5-tile hole in the north hull, so the NE room vents
its smoke to vacuum.

## Editing

Change a yield: edit the `[payloads.studio_*]` row in `config.toml`, restart.
Change a charge (position, payload, timing, `enabled`): edit its `[[entity]]`
row in `level.toml` or the constants in the generator and re-run it (the
generator rewrites `level.toml`, `tilemap.csv` and `diffuse.png`, not this
README).
