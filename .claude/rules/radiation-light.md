# Radiation and light: the sweep, its tables, its planes

Fires on contact with these files. CLAUDE.md's index says what each system is
and who must use it; this file holds the constraints the code cannot say.

## Radiation sweep
- Readers: the temperature fold reads `rad_net_sweep`, the Pass-1 clamp `rad_fluence`, units `rad_flux_sweep` (`src/simulation/exchange.py`). The old `gmap.rad_net` / `rad_flux` / `rad_amb` planes carry nothing: never read them (`rad_net` survives as a dead `step_tail` argument, recorded debt).
- Exact-integer conservative (`Σ rad_net_sweep + Σ rad_flux_sweep + Σ rad_amb_sweep ≡ 0` in int64) and pure integer: the float ratchet pins it at 0.
- The transport step is a parameter, never a fork. Light's is `[light] transport`: `"shear"` shipped (Erik, 2026-09-30), `"step"` the fallback for an absent key, anything else refused at load; bound once in PhysicsRunner.
- Constants are checked in, never computed at load: the per-ordinate constants (`RadiationSweep::ordinate_table`, also the CUDA twin's source, never `std::cos`), the flux direction cosines, and L° (`cpp/src/light_emission_table.inc`, regenerated by `tools/gen_light_table.py --write`).
- The heat currency is `E_FINE_BITS` finer than a heat count (#78): every reader of a sweep plane or an E° value converts ONCE through `fixedpoint::fine_heat_shr` (Python `sweep_ref_q.fine_heat_shr` / `optics_fixed.fine_heat_shr`; display `optics_fixed.dequantize_heat`), never an inline shift.
- Order of change: the Integer reference, then `cpp/src/radiation_sweep.cpp`, then `cpp/src/cuda_radiation_sweep.cu`; `tests/cuda_radiation_sweep_check.py` holds GPU == CPU at tol 0 on every plane and counter. Device books use the unsigned-long-long `atomicAdd` idiom, never float; the `(N, h, w)` launch core counts and masks an illegal env, never throws.
- Fleck damping is ONE exact kit floor-division per cell per tick on the excess only, alpha floored at 0 (Erik, 2026-09-16): never α = ½, never a re-derived reciprocal. The gas arm of L works in the temperature fold's own gas currency (`PhysicsEngine::gas_capacity_q()`), never a second one, and keys on `a_gas`.
- `run()` zeroes its four int64 planes itself and the conductor never wipes them (they hold the last run for the tile inspector). A new output plane joins that zeroing, never a caller's `fill(0)`.
- ONE invocation per path: `PhysicsEngine::run_sweep_` (step 2b, and `relight()`, whose heat outputs land in engine scratch, never a tick's plane); the resident tick's `breach_cuda::resident_tail`.
- Light rides the same traversal as three RGB channels, only when requested (`PhysicsEngine.light_requested`, default off): Kirchhoff per channel with a dark ambient, a body emits nothing, no Fleck, never in the heat books. The per-channel books close exactly and heat is bit-identical with light on or off.
- `RadiationSweep::cone_emission` is THE cone projection (the CUDA twin and the sky's door use it). The sweep's door on cones, sky and `emit_mult` is the only check: no light assembly re-checks it. `emit_mult` scales THERMAL light emission only (the fire's room light), never heat, never a cone; `build_emit_mult` is the one scatter.
- `light_q` is NOT sim-pure until #81 (Light as Game State): its inputs are render state (`renderer/frame_lights.py` `EMITTER_INPUTS`). No game rule may read light before then.

## Emissive table
- The clamp reads `e_ceiling_q`, never `e_inv_q`. Row 31's 0 ceiling below `E°[0]` stays (#78 measured it dormant).
- A table carries its CURRENCY, `fine_bits` (default `E_FINE_BITS`): a resolving-scale table is baked coarse (`fine_bits = 0`); a refused bake leaves the cache as it was.
- The bake BODY is out of line in `cpp/src/emissive_table.cpp` on `/fp:strict`: never a bake in a header (a header-inline bake compiles under `bindings.cpp`'s fast-math and can FMA-contract).
- The live table is baked at `[physics.radiation] rad_scale_derived`, the ONLY emission-calibration key. ONE owner, `engine.emissive` (dials assigned by `src/simulation/physics_runner.py`); `tests/test_emissive_table.py` holds it equal to the reference bake.
- L°, its visible twin, is CHECKED IN (`cpp/src/light_emission_table.inc`, whose static_asserts refuse a table at another currency, glowing at bucket 0, or past `L_TABLE_TOP_MAX`). Readers convert through `optics_fixed.dequantize_light`, never an inline shift. Regenerate with `tools/gen_light_table.py --write`; `tests/test_light_table.py` holds it to the generator.

## Extinction planes
- Every optical interaction the sweep's channels see is a Q16 coefficient on these planes. `0 ≤ a ≤ d ≤ ONE` is an ingress invariant at the materials door (`tests/test_optics_ingress.py`), re-checked by the sweep. Dynamic stamps are MAX, never sums.
- The material share `a` emits and absorbs; the body share `d − a` absorbs and re-emits AMBIENT (Erik's ruling, design row 25). `optics_fixed` RAISES outside [0, 1], never clamps.
- `dyn_heat_atten_q` is digested; `heat_atten_q` deliberately is not (a pure projection of `material`).
- The GAS term is folded in by the sweep's own pre-pass, never stored as a plane. `GasTable.heat_absorb_q16`'s door `[0, 4096]` is int64 headroom, not physics; the density sum is in the TRACE currency through `trace_density_shr`; the gases door refuses an optical coefficient on a bulk row; a thermal solid ignores the gas in its pores.
- Exactly ONE gas absorbs: `[gases.smoke] heat_absorb`, DERIVED in config.toml (Widmann et al. 2003) and re-derived by `tests/test_gas_heat_absorb.py` from the live dials, so moving `soot_yield`, `KG_FUEL_PER_N_O2` or the tile geometry means re-deriving it. Every other gas is 0.0 with its reason (ACCEPTED GAP: band absorbers are not modelled; soot is grey).
- The light channels read their own per-channel twins (`light_atten_q`; `dyn_light_atten_q`, the only dynamic light plane). The light smoke columns get the `[smoke]` dials folded in at the gases door (one owner). Neither light plane is digested before #81.

## Integer reference
- Any change is made in `sweep_ref_q.py` FIRST, its gates re-run, and the C++ written against it, never the other way round. `tests/test_radiation_sweep_reference.py` holds the C++ sweep bit for bit to it, `tests/test_temperature_gas_radiation.py` the C++ fold.
- Its DEFAULT E-table is a RESOLVING scale for the arithmetic gates, read from no config; a number about the SHIPPED game is measured on `E_LIVE`, and `config_dials_match()` guards `RAD_SCALE_LIVE` against `rad_scale_derived`.
- Not engine code: nothing in `src/` or `cpp/` imports it.

## Light accessor
- The renderer, rules and RL read light only through `read_light` / `light_at`, never a sweep plane or a producer. The game REQUESTS light and hands its inputs through `Simulation.set_light` / `relight`; headless runs never do.
- No scalar light field lives on the GameMap: a scalar is `LightView.scalar`, the glow `LightView.glow`.
