# Tuning fire and smoke

*Status: PROVISIONAL. Written 2026-10-22 from the #12 fire + smoke session
(the first run). Every run amends it before merging (see the end).*

Fire and smoke on screen are four systems stacked. Before touching a dial, say
which one the complaint is about:

| Layer | Decides | Dials | Who decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Burning** (sim) | how hot, how long, how strongly a tile burns | `[physics.fire]`, `[physics.combustion]`, material rows | Erik (physics) |
| **Fire brightness** (render + the sweep's light) | how bright a hot tile glows and how much it lights | `[render.blackbody]` | Erik |
| **Smoke amount and lifetime** (sim) | how much soot, how long it lasts, where it goes | `[physics.combustion] soot_yield`, `[gases.smoke] decay`, vents | Erik (physics) |
| **Smoke look** (render) | how the smoke field is drawn | `[render.gas_relief]` and friends: the `smoke-tuning` skill | Claude tunes, Erik judges |


`levels/fire_smoke_studio`, generated by `tools/gen_fire_smoke_studio.py`:
- a row of 5 burning furniture tiles (x 23–27, row 22) and a lone burning tile at (37, 27), lit at 0.5 s;
- the squad north of the fire, so the fire throws their shadows north;
- no lamps (the fire is the light), grey floor;

Change the level by editing the generator's constants and re-running it, never
the level.toml by hand (except a live `q_circ` / `filter` try). One round is
240 ticks = 10 s.

**MUST:** light a fixture with heat alone (`[payloads.studio_ignite]`, heat on
the fuel tile only). *Incident 2026-10-22:* the incendiary ring's flat
`IGNITE_HEAT` turned thin furniture into an ~11 000-unit fireball that killed
the squad 8 tiles away in a second (#99).

## 2. Physics or render? Measure before tuning

```powershell
C:/Users/steen/anaconda3/python.exe tools/fire_smoke_probe.py                 # 90 s table + end profile
C:/Users/steen/anaconda3/python.exe tools/fire_smoke_probe.py --no-vents --seconds 120
C:/Users/steen/anaconda3/python.exe tools/fire_smoke_probe.py --units          # squad HP too
```

It prints, at one burning tile:
- fire, fuel left, tile K;
- the hottest gas nearby;
- the room's smoke total;
- at the end, smoke and wind along a row.

Read it against what Erik sees:
- **"The fire doesn't show."** First A/B the render: smoke relief off
  (`[render.gas_relief] enabled = false`), then glow-through off. If the fire
  is still invisible, it is too dim or too cool, not hidden.
- **"The fire went out."** Look at the fuel column. The fire usually fades
  WITH fuel left (25 % at 120 s): the tile cools faster than it burns, the
  single-temperature solid problem (#68). That is physics, not a render dial.
- **"The smoke doesn't glow."** Smoke has no temperature of its own; it sits
  at its cell's gas temperature. Near a fire that gas peaks at ~740 K, below
  the ~800 K glow floor, because a cell averages a thin flame with a 2.5 m
  column. Not fixable by a dial today (#68). What Erik sees as "glowing smoke"
  is mostly the fire's glow through it: `glow_through_ceiling`.
- **"The smoke doesn't reach the vent / fades too fast."** Check the end
  profile. *Incident 2026-10-22:* the trail was in the sim (a quarter to half
  of the source density at the vent) but drawn as nothing, because
  `cov_tau_ceiling` put the cloud's edge at opaque. And a HEPA vent removes
  smoke faster than slow decay does, which hides a decay change.

## 3. The dials and what they did (2026-10-22 values)

**Fire brightness**, `[render.blackbody]`:
- `kelvin_ref` = 1300: the temperature that reads as brightness 1. At 3000 a
  burning tile drew at 0.003 and the fire was invisible and lit nothing.
- `intensity_exponent` = 2: the curve's steepness. 2 means 3000 K is 19× a fire; 4 was steeper.
- `intensity_max` = 64: the cap. The ramp reaches it near 4800 K.

Colour is black-body throughout; these move only brightness. The sweep's light
table is DERIVED from these three, so after any change:
1. run `tools/gen_light_table.py --write`;
2. rebuild CPU and CUDA (the table is compiled in);
3. restart the game; Ctrl+R does not rebuild it.

`intensity_max · 2^L_FINE_BITS` must stay under 2^44. Today 64 · 2^37 = 2^43,
the limit, so a bigger cap means fewer bits; `tests/test_light_table.py` says so.

**Fire through its own smoke**, `[render.gas_relief]` (live with Ctrl+R):
- `glow_through_ceiling` = 0.2: how much soot hides the glow under it. At 1.0
  the fire's own smoke closed over it and it went dark.

**Smoke lifetime and amount** (physics; restart after a change):
- `[gases.smoke] decay` = 0.001: a ~17 min e-fold.
- `[physics.combustion] soot_yield` = 0.5: smoke units per unit of O2 burned.
  It works as the VISIBILITY dial: the drawn smoke scales with it. The sim's
  smoke optics (`heat_absorb`, `light_absorb`) are derived per gram and must
  be re-derived when it moves (`tests/test_gas_heat_absorb.py` fails until they
  are). The physical soot mass is `gases.SOOT_G_PER_G_WOOD` = 0.015 (cited).

**Smoke drawing**, `[render.gas_relief]`:
- `cov_tau_ceiling` = 0.8: the optical depth where the soot cloud's edge sits.
  Lower draws thinner smoke as cloud.
- `haze_ceiling` = 0.3: how much thin smoke shows outside that edge.

The rest of the relief is the `smoke-tuning` skill.

**Air:**
- Drag (`[physics.eos] k_drag` 0.01 / `k_drag2` 0.225) was re-checked. 0.1 was
  tried and reverted.
- The heat-only grenade (`frag_standard`) pushes little air. That belongs to #31,
  not to drag.
- Vents: `q_circ` (16 in the studio) and the duct `filter` (`hepa_basic`
  removes smoke, `derelict` only moves it).

## 4. Landing it

- Erik edits `config.toml` in the checkout he plays from. Read `git diff
  config.toml` there, and carry his values into the session branch WITH their
  reason in the comment (what it looked like before, his words).
- Commit every try he is shown. Not on the integration line: use the session
  worktree, then `merge --no-ff`.
- A `[render.blackbody]` change means the light table and both builds. A
  decay, soot or drag change moves the golden: re-baseline once, with its
  sole cause shown.
- Full suite before merging. It's feel-adjacent, so Erik has seen it (he was
  at the screen).
- Close the game before rebuilding the main checkout: a running game holds the
  module and the link fails.
