## Reproduce

Blender 4.5.10 LTS, bundled Python. From `art/characters/`:

    bash charkit/run.sh stocky_agent_claude --turn --beauty all --samples 128 --save   # about 2.5 minutes
    bash charkit/run.sh stocky_agent_claude --draft --turn                            # about 12 seconds

Outputs: `previews/` (tracked), `source/stocky_agent.blend` (gitignored, regenerated by
`--save`) and `source/mesh_stats.json` (tracked). Kit, studio and build driver: `art/characters/charkit/`.

## What is modelled

1.68 m to the helmet top; a heavy-set, strong woman's build (hips wider than shoulders, full
bust, thick thighs that meet), neutral A-pose, empty relaxed hands.

- Bodysuit: one welded charcoal surface from ankle to neck with orange piping (two short lines
  on the belly, one down each outer thigh), stitched seams, ribbed stretch at the neck, inner
  elbow and knee hollow; a black armband on the left arm.
- Armour (white, smooth, rounded — the artwork's look): cuirass with rolled rim, collar ring,
  dome pauldrons, forearm gauntlets, knee caps, knee-high boot shafts with a row of studs,
  a back unit with vents and hatch.
- Helmet: boxy, flat-topped shell with an open dark face, a white visor housing across the
  brow carrying a dark slit and ONE blue lens (on the right), respirator, ear housings.
- Gear: belt with white buckle and four pouches.

## Measured

`source/mesh_stats.json`: 60 mesh objects, 491,702 source triangles, 1,146,484 after
modifiers (suit 270 k, boots 92 k, helmet 69 k, armour 31 k, gloves 27 k, gear 2 k source).
No silhouette score, for the same reason as the sleek agent: a single view of a posed figure
with props is not a measurable target. The gallery shows the artwork beside a straight-on render.

## Not done, and known weaknesses

- No UV maps, no bake, no rig, no skin weights, no export, no engine test.
- A high-resolution source, not a game mesh. Parts overlap rather than weld, except the suit.
- Against the artwork: the pose is neutral, not the artwork's weight-on-one-leg stance; the
  cuirass has a straight lower edge and invented panel lines; the chest lettering and small
  markings are replaced by plain shapes; the flat, graphic shading of the illustration is a
  lit, physically shaded surface here.
- The lens is a glossy blue surface, not a light: nothing on the model emits.
- Materials are Cycles node graphs and do not carry to the game's shader.
