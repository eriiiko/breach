Hands (the reviewer's first item)

One closed surface per hand (charkit/parts.py::bare_hand): a palm narrow at the wrist and widest at the knuckles, a rounded heel and a thumb pad, a two-segment thumb from the side of the palm's base, four fingers of different lengths in three tapering phalanges with a slight knuckle and a rounded tip, relaxed curl and a little spread. The fingers grow out of the palm through smooth fillets (webbing), no notch or step.

Shoulder and arm

Torso and arm are one surface over the shoulder: the smooth union of the body and sleeve lofts, meshed on its own and bounded by seam lines (in front the line from the collar to the armpit, behind a raglan seam outside the blade insert, a short seam under the arm, and on the arm the drawn seam at the foot of the deltoid), so every join lies under a piping cord. No armhole ring. The sleeve is refitted: a slim wrist, the forearm swelling under the elbow, a narrowing and a point at the elbow.
