Hip shapes, side by side

Three shapes built from table values only, in a matte grey so the gloss hides nothing, front and side, waist to knee. The three drawings vs the model

Silhouette overlap (IoU), default build, per height band (above the waist / waist to knee / knees down). Front 0.947 / 0.870 / 0.900; side 0.918 / 0.917 / 0.869; back 0.913 / 0.837 / 0.832. The waist-to-knee band is narrower than the drawings on purpose (sheet_hips: front 0.947, side 0.959, back 0.886). Knees down: the front and back drawings place the lower legs 1.8 cm apart and draw a high heel; the build splits the difference and wears the low heel asked for.
