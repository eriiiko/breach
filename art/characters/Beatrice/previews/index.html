Beatrice — body, suit, hands, boots (stage 3: second correction round)

Built from Python alone on the shared character kit (charkit/bodysuit.py, Blender 4.5, Cycles) from the tables in scripts/beatrice.py, fitted to three drawings: front, side and back. The head is a bald placeholder for the face-and-hair stage. Character only, no props. This round answers review_stage2.md: new hands, the shoulder and arm as one surface, a sleek ankle boot, clean teardrop inserts and a dark-grey thigh panel, the S-curve of the back, a fine zip, layered knee pads, thin cuffs, seams that end on other edges. See PROVENANCE.md.

Hands (the reviewer's first item)

One closed surface per hand (charkit/parts.py::bare_hand): a palm narrow at the wrist and widest at the knuckles, a rounded heel and a thumb pad, a two-segment thumb from the side of the palm's base, four fingers of different lengths in three tapering phalanges with a slight knuckle and a rounded tip, relaxed curl and a little spread. The fingers grow out of the palm through smooth fillets (webbing), no notch or step.

Shoulder and arm

Torso and arm are one surface over the shoulder: the smooth union of the body and sleeve lofts, meshed on its own and bounded by seam lines (in front the line from the collar to the armpit, behind a raglan seam outside the blade insert, a short seam under the arm, and on the arm the drawn seam at the foot of the deltoid), so every join lies under a piping cord. No armhole ring. The sleeve is refitted: a slim wrist, the forearm swelling under the elbow, a narrowing and a point at the elbow.

The three drawings vs the model

Silhouette overlap (IoU), default build, per height band (above the waist / waist to knee / knees down): front 0.946 / 0.871 / 0.908 (whole 0.903); side 0.918 / 0.915 / 0.856; back 0.911 / 0.848 / 0.813. The waist-to-knee band is narrower than the drawings on purpose (the owner's slimmer hips). Knees down: the front and back drawings place the lower legs apart and draw a high heel; the build splits the difference and wears the low heel asked for.
