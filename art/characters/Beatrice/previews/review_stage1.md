# Beatrice, stage 1 — review of the body build

Two lists, written independently on 2026-10-04 from the same pictures and references: the
orchestrator's (written first, before the reviewer reported) and a separate Opus reviewer's. The
comparison at the end is the experiment the `scripted-character` skill asks for in lesson 1.

## Orchestrator's list (written before reading the reviewer's)

1. **Torso is a stiff tube.** Square, wide, horizontal shoulders with a visible ring where the arm
   meets the body (`hero.jpg`, `turnaround.png` front); the drawings show sloping shoulders and a
   soft S-curve from ribs to waist to hip (`without_hair.png`).
2. **Side profile is nearly flat** (`turnaround.png` side, `side.jpg`): straight back, almost no
   seat, a small high cone for the bust, straight tube legs. `without_hair_side.png` shows a lumbar
   curve, a fuller and lower bust, a round seat, and curved thigh and calf lines. The seat must come
   down from the drawing (the owner's steer) but not to nothing: this overshoots.
3. **The back is plain and partly wrong** (`hero_back.jpg`, `turnaround.png` back): the drawing
   (`without_hair_back.png`) has a zip down the spine, mesh panels at the shoulder blades and the
   waist sides, the outer-thigh panels seen from behind, knee backs and leg seams. The model has a
   centre seam and two curved seams, plus a pointed crease at the top of the seat that reads as an
   artefact.
4. **Legs read as sticks with knobs:** thin straight thighs, a knee pad that is a smooth blob with
   a tail, and a calf that bulges below it (`hero.jpg`, `legs.jpg`). The drawings have a continuous
   thigh-to-calf line and a shaped, seamed knee shield.
5. **The two hip variants are hard to tell apart** in `shape_vs_concept.jpg`; the difference the
   owner asked to judge is about 9 % and does not read at that size.
6. **Mesh panels look like stickers** with a moiré, wood-grain pattern, and their shapes are off:
   straight bars at the shoulders (drawn: thin curved slits), big tongues at the waist (drawn:
   narrow crescents), ovals on the thighs (drawn: long panels starting at the hip).
7. **Gloss is chrome-like:** hard white rectangles of the studio lights, and a dark flat plate on
   the upper chest (`turnaround.png` front). The drawings have a softer, broader sheen.
8. **Arms are straight tubes** with no forearm taper; **hands are large, pale and spread**, thumb
   sticking out (`hero.jpg`); the drawn hands are small and relaxed.
9. **Boots are a court shoe with a thin heel and a separate cuff** (`side.jpg`, `hero_back.jpg`);
   the drawing has an ankle boot with a block heel, and the owner has since asked for a LOW heel
   (about 3 cm).
10. Small: a slight crumple at the crotch weld; faint white specks near the right hip panel.

Good, keep: overall height proportions and limb lengths against the front view; collar and front
zip; the panel seams on the front of the torso; the palette variant mechanism.

## Opus reviewer's list (independent; it opened all twelve renders)

1. **The back was invented and does not match** (`torso_back.jpg`, `hero_back.jpg` vs
   `without_hair_back.png`): no centre-back zip, no mesh panels on the shoulder blades and rib
   sides, no curved seams from the shoulders past the seat; an "arrowhead" panel over the sacrum
   that no drawing shows; the outer-thigh panels show only as a sliver from behind.
2. **The seat is too flat; the slimming went too far behind** (`hero_back.jpg`, `side.jpg`): a flat
   block running straight into the thighs, no fold under the seat; a straight lower back. From the
   front the torso reads boyish and rectangular; the concept still has a clear waist with hips
   wider than it — her waist is too thick for her hips.
3. **Thigh mesh panels: wrong shape, stuck on** (`legs.jpg`, `hero.jpg`): big oval pads wrapping to
   the front with a raised rim and a chevron moiré; the drawings show a narrow strip down the outer
   thigh set in between seams.
4. **The seam lines are missing** (`torso.jpg`, `legs.jpg` vs `without_hair.png`): the drawing has
   continuous piping from each shoulder, round the chest mesh, past the waist mesh to the hip and
   down the thigh to the knee; the model has the crotch V and fragments. The torso mesh pieces float
   as separate lozenges with thick rims. The side drawing shows two diagonal mesh bands at ribs and
   waist; the model shows lumps.
5. **Knee pads read as decals:** flat discs toward the inner knee with a teardrop tail, a lump in
   profile; the drawings show a rounded kneecap framed by seams.
6. **Hands:** oversized, flat, mitten-like with fused fingers, thumb out at about 90 degrees, a dark
   line across the fingers.
7. **Boots:** the shaft is a separate straight tube wider than the leg with a step, a gap and a
   notch; the drawn boot continues the leg line to mid-shin; the toe is square (drawn: almond); the
   feet splay.
8. **Collar and zip:** the collar is a tall straight tube; the zip pull sticks out under the chin as
   a tab; the zip is a flat bright strip with no teeth and reads as tape.
9. **Upper body:** broad square shoulders with puffy caps where the sleeves meet the body, thick
   upper arms, a long neck, a bust flatter than drawn.
10. **Material:** chrome or hard plastic, not latex; hard-edged mirror reflections, dark smears and
    reddish specks mid-chest, lighter grey bands on the shins.
11. **Artefacts:** a diagonal crease on the upper back near the left shoulder; a ridge where the
    sleeve meets the shoulder; pinched creases at the crotch; a vertical line down the shins; a cuff
    step that is barely there (drawn: a distinct band).
12. **Posture:** stiffly upright, no curve in the lower back, pelvis not tilted.

Good, do not touch: overall height, leg-to-torso ratio and the pose; the placement of the chest
mesh and the zip ending near the navel; the crotch V seam; calf shape and taper to the ankle; the
colour parameter (crimson reads well); at a distance the black gloss reads as the intended suit.

## The comparison (the skill's lesson-1 experiment)

- Everything the orchestrator found on the model, the reviewer found too: the invented back, the
  flat side and seat, the tube torso and square shoulders, sticker-like mesh panels, blob knee pads,
  the hands, the boots, the chrome gloss, the crotch crumple.
- The reviewer found more: the zip without teeth and the pull tab, the collar, the missing
  continuous piping (the orchestrator had listed the front seams as good), the long neck and flat
  bust, four further artefacts. It opened all twelve renders; the orchestrator opened four.
- The orchestrator alone: the two hip variants cannot be told apart in `shape_vs_concept.jpg` (a
  presentation point the reviewer was not asked about).
- One disagreement: the calves (orchestrator: bulging below the knee; reviewer: good).
- Result, on this one character: a separate Opus reviewer given the pictures, the references and
  the owner's decisions caught at least as much as the orchestrator did.
