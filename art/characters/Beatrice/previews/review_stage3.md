# Beatrice, after the second correction round — review, hips first

2026-10-04. The owner looked at `shape_vs_concept.jpg` and the turnaround after round 1 and said:
he wants the "one step slimmer" shape; he does not like "the sharp edges on her hips" (all three
shapes have them); the hips are the most important part of this model, and if they cannot be got
right the character is paused. An Opus reviewer then examined the round-2 renders, hips first.

## The hips (reviewer)

**Front** (grey panels of `shape_vs_concept.jpg`, `sheet_vs_reference.png`, `hero.jpg`):
- A real SILHOUETTE corner on both sides at crotch height (about 0.75 m), where the hip meets the
  thigh. Above it the waist-to-hip outline is an almost straight diagonal; below it the outline
  turns sharply into a near-vertical thigh line. All three shapes have it.
- The waist pinch is far too weak, so there is no reverse curve feeding into the hip and the flare
  reads as a cone. The drawings and the concept pinch hard at the navel; from there the outline
  swells in ONE continuous convex curve, widest just below the crotch (at the hip bone, where the
  thigh starts), then eases into the thigh.
- Below the corner the thigh narrows straight to the knee, like a carrot.
- The outer-thigh panel seam about 1 cm inside the outline adds a second hard vertical line.
- The crotch is a narrow pointed inverted V with inner thighs bulging just under it; the drawing has
  a soft rounded gap.

**Side** (`side_vs_reference.png`, `side.jpg`, lower row of `shape_vs_concept.jpg`):
- The buttock is a small bump stuck on high, with a hard shelf and notch at the gluteal fold; the
  outline steps in there and then runs dead straight down the back of the thigh.
- The front profile is a flat plane from belly to knee, with no forward swell of the thigh.
- The drawing has one S-curve: a deeper lower-back hollow, a full round seat whose lowest point
  blends into the hamstring line, a convex front thigh.

**Back** (`back_vs_reference.png`, `torso_back.jpg`, `hero_back.jpg`):
- The same crotch-level corner; the waist-to-hip outline is straight.
- The seat seams draw a hard "heart" outline that reads as a panel edge.
- A flat highlight plane on each outer hip shows a faceted surface rather than a curved one.

## Still wrong elsewhere (reviewer, most important first)
1. Waist and torso: barely pinched, tubular from ribcage to hip (half the hip problem).
2. Boots: the shaft still a stiff cylinder with a stepped rim, wider than the leg; flat plate sole,
   box heel, long flat toe.
3. Knee pads: flat double-outlined discs toward the inner knee with a strap band.
4. Outer-thigh mesh panel: a jagged, sawtooth lower edge.
5. Upper sleeve: a horizontal seam ring with a V-notch, reading as an armour joint.
6. Zips: the back zip ends in a square tab at mid-back; the front one in a dangling square pull.
7. Hands: the back of the hand lumpy and swollen, fingers stubby, the hand too big.
8. Waist mesh insets: thin floating teardrops where the drawing has broad bands.
9. Deltoid: a dent or lump on the far shoulder.

Good, do not touch: chest form and breast seams; collar and front zip line; shoulder mesh slots;
lower legs and calves; overall proportions and pose; the latex material; the wrist cuffs.

Verdict: apart from the hips, not ready; the waist pinch first, then the boots.
