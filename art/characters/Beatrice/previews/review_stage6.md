# Beatrice, round B1f (leg gap, side profile, front flare) — independent review

2026-10-06. I judged by eye only, from the pictures in `previews/`. I opened every listed picture whole.
The pelvis pictures I also cropped 2–3x, and contrast-stretched or equalized them where shading was in
question. References: `concept.jfif`, `good-body-silhouette.jfif` and the three `without_hair*` drawings.
I read no builder notes. Positions are given as places in the named picture.

## 1. Verdict

The leg gap is fixed. The thighs now meet under the crotch and part as a slim, pointed-top lens with
convex sides, front and back, and the slot is gone. That was the main point of stage 5. The front is no
longer a cone. Two things still stand between this and a finished pelvis. The narrowest waist is now a
tight turn that shows as a hard horizontal line across the waist, in the matte shading and as a "belt"
in the gloss (`torso.jpg`, `hero.jpg`). And from the side she is still mostly a tube: the outline moved
only 5–7 mm. The pelvis is good enough to show the owner as "the hips round". His first remark will most
likely be the line or pinch at the waist in the glossy front. His second will be that the side is still
flat.

## 3. New defects, worst first

**N1. The narrowest waist is a tight turn with a hard line around it** — SHAPE, showing as SURFACE.
- Matte: in `hips.jpg` front, side and three-quarter back, the shading steps from dark to light across the
  whole body at the narrowest waist, over a few mm, like a ring. It is the sharpest edge in the picture
  with the contrast raised.
- Outline: in `hips_before_after.jpg` "now" front and back, the gray outline and the drawn lines bend at
  the waist with a small radius, a soft "X" where the torso's inward taper meets the hip's outward flare.
  The picture's own number says the same (worst turn 11.3 deg/cm in front).
- Gloss: in `torso.jpg` and `hero.jpg` a straight, crisp horizontal reflection edge runs across the whole
  abdomen at that height, and below it a dark band. It reads as a belt.
- `shape_vs_silhouette.jpg` B1f shows the cause: the rib cage comes down to the waist as a straight cone
  and the hip leaves it as another straight cone.
- The references: in `good-body-silhouette.jfif` and the concept, the waist is one long, smooth curve with
  no line across it. Its narrowest part stretches over several cm of height, and the reflections on the
  suit wrap around it.

**N2. Straight flanks from waist to knee in front** — SHAPE.
- `hips.jpg` front and `hips_before_after.jpg` "now" front: from the waist the hip outline is a nearly
  straight diagonal out to its widest point a little above crotch height.
- From there the outer thigh runs almost vertical to the knee.
- The hip therefore reads as a trapezoid with a softened top corner, not a round. The convex roundness
  `flare_full` gives (its outline bulges outward from about navel height) is missing from the default.
- The references show a round hip that is already convex just below the waist and tapers into the thigh.

**N3. Glossy contact line and nick under the seam V** — SURFACE.
- `crotch.jpg` front glossy: a thin bright vertical line runs about 2 cm from the seam V's tip down to
  where the gap opens, and a small dark rounded "comma" sits at the V tip on one side.
- The same line shows in `legs.jpg` and `hero.jpg` crotch at their full resolution. It reads as a
  short seam or nick at the crotch.
- At the hero framing it is small, but it sits exactly where the owner looks first. The references show
  the suit running unbroken into the thigh contact.

**N4. The lens's lower end joins the old V with a slight hinge** — SHAPE, mild.
- `hips_before_after.jpg` "now" front and back, and `hips.jpg` front, inner contour of the viewer's left
  thigh: about 4–5 cm below the cusp the convex lens wall meets the straight old V wall.
- At that join the contour turns slightly concave before running straight, a faint S.
- Only visible in the matte front/back. The references widen smoothly toward the knee.

**N5. A horizontal line at cusp height in the matte three-quarter front** — SURFACE, minor.
- `bands_contrast.jpg` high-pass: a short bright streak on the near thigh beside the crotch, and a
  hairline running across the whole far thigh at the same height.
- In the plain `hips.jpg` three-quarter front, only the short streak beside the crotch is faintly visible.
- Not visible in any glossy picture.

**N6. The gap's top is a needle, the reference's a blunt opening** — SHAPE, does not matter by eye.
- `lens.jpg`: the reference's first light is a small blunt pentagon already a couple of cm wide under a
  broad U of the crotch. The model's light starts as a hair-thin point and widens slowly.
- In matte close-ups that point is the sharpest feature of the pelvis.
- In every glossy picture (`hero.jpg`, `legs.jpg`, `sheet_vs_reference.png`) the crotch reads naturally:
  thighs meeting, then parting. I would not change it for its own sake. If the tip is touched for N4,
  a slightly blunter tip would be closer to the reference.

**N7. Front zip pull sticks out of the belly contour** — DETAIL.
`side.jpg` (and `c_side` crops) at belly height: the square pull at the bottom of the front zip stands
off the front outline as a small dot. It should lie flat.

## 4. Front shape: recommendation

What the pictures show for each shape:
- **stage4** (`shape_vs_silhouette.jpg`, `hips_before_after.jpg` row 1): a soft column. Waist and hip barely
  differ, the sides are near straight, it reads as a cone. No corner.
- **b1e** (row 2): the same with a little flare near crotch height. Still a cone with a late, short rise
  to full width. No corner, but straight-sided.
- **B1f default** (row 4 / "B1f"): an hourglass at last. The flare starts at the narrowest waist. But the
  waist turn is tight (N1, line across the waist in matte and gloss) and the flanks below are straight
  (N2). The waist reads a little pinched in matte, and as a belt line in gloss.
- **flare_full** (row 3): the roundest hip. The outline is convex from about navel height, which is closest
  to the frontal reference's hip. But the waist is a crisp corner on both sides in front, and in
  `hips_before_after.jpg` flare_full side there is a hard horizontal shading line all round the waist.
  It reads clearly pinched, like a corset seam.

**Recommendation: take the B1f default.** It is the only one with an S and without a visible corner in the
outline. Do not take flare_full: its corner is visible in the plain matte front and side without any
contrast stretch.

A step between the two would not help as it stands. The corner grows with the flare because the problem
sits at the waist itself: two straight tapers meeting. A middle step would get a middle corner. Round the
waist turn first (spread the narrowest part over more height, so that the rib-cage taper curves into the
hip flare: correction 1 below). After that, more of flare_full's convexity can be taken. With the waist
rounded, I would expect about three-quarters of the way to flare_full to look best. Judge that by eye on
the matte front and the glossy front, not by number.

## 5. Good, do not change

- **The leg gap.** The thighs meet, then part as a lens, front and back, with no slot, no ridge and no
  arrow highlight (`crotch.jpg`, `hips_before_after.jpg` "now"). This is what the owner asked for.
- The soft contact hollow above the cusp in matte. It reads as touching flesh, not a crease.
- The glossy front crotch at normal viewing distance (`sheet_vs_reference.png` model front,
  `legs.jpg`). It reads natural and matches the frontal reference's feel.
- No corner at crotch height or on the hips anywhere, in any view, matte or glossy.
- The overall hip width and size ("one step slimmer").
- The back view's single convex line from waist over hip into thigh, and the short soft cleft
  (`hips.jpg` back).
- The two-lobe split at the bottom of the glossy seat highlight (`hero_back.jpg`). Build on it.
- The side under the seat: no notch, a smooth run into the back of the thigh.

## 6. Corrections for one more round, in order

1. **Round the waist turn (N1), then take more flare (N2).** Spread the narrowest waist over more height,
   so that the torso's taper curves smoothly into the hip and no line or belt shows across the waist,
   matte or glossy (`torso.jpg`, `hero.jpg`, `hips.jpg` side are the checks). Then move the hip flare
   toward flare_full's convex hip (about three-quarters) with the widest width unchanged, and let the
   outer thigh taper slightly toward the knee instead of the vertical column.
2. **Side profile: twice the change this round made.** Give the front of the thigh a convex swell along its
   length, not only under the groin. Give the seat an under-curve: a soft lower edge that tucks into the
   back of the thigh, with no notch. That edge is also what is missing in the back and three-quarter back
   views. Deepen the lumbar slightly. Keep the hip width, and stay well inside the side drawing.
3. **Clean the crotch details (N3, N4, N5).** Remove the bright contact line and the dark comma under the
   seam V in the gloss, smooth the hinge where the lens meets the lower gap, and clear the cusp-height
   hairline and the 0.80 m band.

**Is it worth a round?**
- Correction 1 is worth doing before the owner sees her. It is a single local change, and the waist line
  is the most visible fault left in the glossy pictures he will look at.
- Correction 2 has moved only 5–7 mm in each of the last two rounds. How much seat and thigh she should
  have from the side is a call for the owner: the side drawing is known to be too full, and the concept is
  three-quarter. Show him the pelvis after correction 1 and ask him that question, rather than run another
  blind round.
- Correction 3 is polish that can ride along with either.
- After that, leave the pelvis and take the body's other parts.
