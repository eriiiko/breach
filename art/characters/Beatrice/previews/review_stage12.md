# Beatrice, rounds B2c (hair) and B2d (face finish, latex sheen): independent review

2026-10-22. I judged by eye from the pictures in `previews/`. For before/after I took review 11's set from git
(`3292a4c`). I read no builder notes. The face and hair references are `concept-face-front-v1.png` and
`concept-face-side-v1.png`. For style, the whole figure and how the latex is painted I used `concept.jfif`.
Crops and zooms are in `C:/tmp/beatrice_rev12/v/`. The ones I name are `fva_front.jpg` / `fva_prof.jpg`
(face_vs_art row 1 at 1.33×), `grid_face.jpg` (drawing | model front, 20 px grid), `ft0/30/60/90.jpg`

Measurements: on `face_vs_art.jpg` the drawing and the model share one 420 × 420 px tile frame, so I measured
in tile pixels. I measured hair widths as runs of pixels that differ from the background (summed RGB
difference > 45), with the same script on the old and the new picture. I took colours as 5 × 5 or 7 × 7 means.
Suit tone is luminance percentiles in fixed boxes on `posed_vs_concept.jpg` (concept bust x 330–820,
y 730–930; render bust x 1430–1780, y 730–920; upper chest boxes beside the zip). My boxes are not review 11's,

## 1. Review 11's list, item by item

1. **HAIR, fringe and side locks: much better.** The comb of equal teeth is gone. The fringe is now clumped
   locks with a central lock that drops between the eyes to the lash line, and the forehead shows only in
   slivers (`ft0.jpg`, `fva_front.jpg`). Side locks fall in front of the ears and past the jaw, so from the
   front the ears only peek out. The hair width now nearly matches the drawing. At the eye line (tile y 200) it
   is 336 px against the drawing's 358 (94 %; it was 244, 68 %). At the chin (y 300) it is 379 against 391
   (97 %; it was 243). What is left is in section 3.
2. **FACE, the eyes' drawing: better, not done.** Lid shadow over the top of the iris and the white: fixed, and
   it is the biggest single gain (`eye_front_b2d.jpg`). Greyer white: fixed. Iris larger and darker at the rim:
   fixed. Crease and lower lashes: there. Lash mass: **not fixed.** The upper lid is still the same flat black
   ribbon, now with a thin fur of fine hairs along its top edge. The drawing's lash mass is brown, about twice
   as thick, hangs over the iris and fans out past the outer corner (`eye_front_art.jpg`). Corners: unchanged.
   At the outer end the white still stops in a vertical, slightly ragged edge where the lower-lid rim turns up
   to meet the flick (`eye_front_b2d.jpg`, right of the iris). At 60° a notch tears the outer corner, and the
   liner's inner end is a thick black vertical block (`eye_60_b2d.jpg`).
3. **FACE, brows: fixed.** They are thicker and darker, with hair strokes and a heavier inner end. At 30° and 60°
   they give her a serious look (`ft30.jpg`, `eye_60_b2d.jpg`). At the concept's framing they are still fainter
   than the concept's (`pvc_face2.jpg`), which is polish.
4. **LOOK, latex sheen: better, half done.** The ragged upper-chest blobs are **gone**. The upper-chest 90th
   percentile fell from 217 to 80. There is now a middle tone. The bust's 75th / 90th / 95th percentiles are
   53 / 102 / 121, against 59 / 60 / 71 before and the concept's 130 / 196 / 211. So the sheen is at about half
   the concept's value. It is also cloudy rather than shaped (section 4). The flank stripe is greyer but still
   one continuous line.
5. **FACE, cheek groove: better.** In `zeb_cmp.jpg` the 30° stripes on the near cheek now run evenly where B2c's
   pinched. At 60° a small Y-shaped kink remains near the jaw corner. In the toon pictures the near cheek still
   shows soft light and dark streaks (`ft30.jpg`, `head.jpg`, right of the eye). They now follow the side lock,
   so I read them as its blurred shadow (item 7), not as the groove.
6. **FACE, paint: mixed.** The mouth is fixed: a line, a tinted lower lip with a gloss, and slight corners
   (`face_closeup.jpg`, "nose and mouth"). The blush is fixed, maybe overshot. Its drop in green from base skin
   is now 25–33, against the drawing's 20–27, on a paler skin: (242, 205, 187) against (230, 189, 173). The
   nose is **worse in effect** (section 2).
7. **LOOK, shadows: unchanged.** The fringe shadow on the forehead and upper lids is still soft, blurry
   vertical streaks (`eye_front_b2d.jpg` top, `fc` toon eyes). The same streaks now also sit on the near cheek
   from the side locks. The under-chin is still light: (209–212, 157–167, 142–154) against the drawing's
   (169–178, 125, 109).
8. **OTHER, head pose in the concept framing: unchanged.** She is still upright and frontal
   (`posed_vs_concept.jpg`).
9. **OTHER, hairless pictures: fixed.** The ghost ear is gone. The ragged terracotta band is now a clean,
   smooth shadow step behind a round ear (`bald_prof.jpg`). The hairless views can now be used to judge the
   skin.
10. **LOOK, knee pads: better.** The top of each pad now has a soft cap and a white rim, so it reads as a raised
    pad (`knee.jpg`). The body of the pad is a flat, lighter, matte grey oval, so the two still look like
    different materials.

## 2. The face against the close-ups

**Proportions** are unchanged and still right (`grid_face.jpg`). Eyes, nose, mouth and chin sit within a few
pixels of the front drawing, and the profile outline fits (`fva_prof.jpg`).

**Eyes.** At portrait distance the blank stare is mostly gone. The lid shadow and the darker iris give the gaze
weight (`ft0.jpg`, `head.jpg`). Up close the eye is still more open and rounder than the drawing's. The iris
top only touches the liner, while in the drawing the lash mass covers its top fifth. The liner is black where
the drawing's lashes are dark brown. The fine lashes on top read as fur along the ribbon, not as a lash mass.
The lower lashes are evenly spaced ticks, regular like a comb. The corners are as in section 1, item 2.

**Brows.** Good (section 1, item 3).

**Nose: the worst thing in the face now.** From the front the highlight is a flat, hard-edged, pure-white
trapezoid with a notch, like a tooth or a sticker (`fva_front.jpg`, `grid_face.jpg`, `eyes_b2d.jpg` front B2d,
bottom left). The drawing's is a soft, warm glow that brightens toward a small glint. The highlight is the
brightest spot on the face (254, 237, 229), so at the concept's framing it is the first thing the eye lands on
(`pvc_face2.jpg`). At 30° and 60° the builder's flag is confirmed. A large flat white patch sits on the nose's
flank and bridge (`ft30.jpg`, `ft60.jpg`, `face_closeup.jpg` "her left eye, three-quarter"). The concept's
three-quarter face has only a small soft spot on the tip. The nostril strokes are right in size, but a little
dark and curly.

**Mouth.** Good. In profile there is a slight pout now (`face_closeup.jpg`, "mouth, from her right").

**Blush.** As strong as the drawing's, or a little stronger (section 1, item 6). On her paler skin, and with
the white nose between the cheeks, it reads as flushed at portrait distance and in the posed framing
(`head.jpg`, `pvc_face2.jpg`). I would take it down by about a fifth rather than more.

**Cheeks, skin tone, outlines.** The cheek is smooth enough. The skin is a step lighter than both drawings
throughout. Next to the concept, which is in shade, she looks pale (`pvc_face2.jpg`). The outlines are still
good.

## 3. The hair framing the face

The B2c hair is the round's big win. From the front it now frames the face as the drawing does. It is not yet
the drawing's hair.

- **Silhouette: a hood.** The crown is a smooth dome, as wide as the drawing's at y 100 (314 against 308). It
  then pinches in at temple and brow level, to 294 against 336 (88 %) at y 155, before the curtains flare out.
  Around the temples an outline runs as a rounded rim, so the head reads as a hood or a helmet with ear-muffs
  (`ft0.jpg`, `pf_fronthead.jpg`, `posed_hotlight.jpg` toon panel). The drawing widens steadily from the crown
  down. At the back, the same rim makes a waist where the crown shell meets the hanging sheet, and the back of
  the crown has a small V notch (`hb_back.jpg`).
- **Part and crown.** No part. The drawing's part is just right of centre, and strands sweep from it. The angel
  ring is now a sawtooth band, the right idea, but it is drawn as flat grey-mauve blocks (71, 52, 48) that read
  pasted on rather than glossy (`ft0.jpg`, `ft90.jpg`).
- **Colour.** One uniform dark brown. The hanging hair is (38–48, 26–35, 26–34). That is the drawing's
  **shadow** side, (47–71, 37–62, 37–66). Its lit side is warm ash, (110–160, 86–130, 80–120), and goes to pale
  beige (201, 195, 196) at the edge. The concept does the same on her left. At any distance this is the largest
  colour difference left on the whole character.
- **Fringe.** The outer locks stop at the brow line. The drawing's reach the upper lash line and overlap the
  lids. The tips are all the same wide-based triangle, like shark teeth. The drawing's end in thin, uneven
  wisps.
- **Ears in profile.** The ear is round (`bald_prof.jpg`). With hair, at 60° and 90°, the cap's edge cuts across
  its top on a diagonal, and the visible ear ends in a point at the back: an elf ear again (`ft60.jpg`,
  `ft90.jpg`, `pf_sidehead.jpg`, `posed_full.jpg` side). At the side view of `posed_full.jpg` it is visible at full
  figure.
- **Chest locks** are tubes with a dark outline on each side, so they read as cords or straps over the bust
  (`pf_fronthead.jpg`, `head.jpg`, `torso.jpg`).
- **Hanging hair** is evenly parallel strands with near-even ends. The drawings show separated, tapered, wavy
  ends and a few loose strands (`hb_back.jpg` against `with_hair_back.png`).
- **Stray strands.** At 30° two or three thin dark lines stick out sideways at the chin, like wires (`ft30.jpg`,
  `head.jpg` left, `head_size.jpg` 30° tiles).

## 4. The latex against `concept.jfif`

**At full figure it works and is better than B2c** (`hero.jpg`, `turnaround.png`, `posed_full.jpg`). It reads
as wet black latex without the paint spills. Two things still set it apart from the concept's painting
(`pvc_torso.jpg`):

- **The sheen is half as bright and has no shape.** The concept paints one big, bright, graded highlight on the
  upper outer curve of each breast and on the shoulder caps. It turns crisply to dark below, so the bust reads
  round. The render's middle tone is grey, cloudy patches. They sit on the upper chest and across the bust tops
  wherever the camera-space lobe falls, and do not follow the form, so they read as haze or dust. The upper
  chest is now **lighter** than the concept's (median 57 against 37), where the concept keeps it its darkest
  part. The base under the bust is lifted to (37, 37, 39) against the concept's (31, 28, 31). Overall the torso
  has less light-to-dark contrast than in B2c and the concept, and the bust looks flatter.
- **The long stripes.** The flank streak from ribs to hip and the white stripe down the outer arm are each still
  one continuous, even line (`torso.jpg`, `hero.jpg`). The concept's rims break up.

Knee pads: section 1, item 10.

## 5. The whole figure in the concept framing

`posed_vs_concept.jpg`, `pvc_face2.jpg`. She is clearly closer than after B2b. The face is the same kind of
drawing as the concept's, the hair now frames it, and there are no paint blobs. What dominates the remaining
gap, in order:

1. **The hair's colour and shape.** A uniform dark brown hood against the concept's lit, ash-toned, center-parted
   hair with loose strands across the face.
2. **Pose and expression.** The concept's head is tilted and the chin is down, so she looks up from under knitted
   brows. The render stands upright and frontal. This costs nothing in modelling.
3. **The white nose sticker and the strong blush.** They pull the eye to the middle of the face.
4. **The latex value structure.** Half-strength, cloudy sheen against the concept's bright, form-following
   highlights.
5. The lash mass, at this distance a smaller effect.

## 6. Ranked corrections for the next rounds

1. **HAIR: colour and crown.** A lit side in warm ash to pale beige with strand lines, a part just right of
   centre with strands sweeping from it, and a glossier angel ring. **Worth a round.**
2. **HAIR: silhouette.** Remove the pinch and rim at the temples, so the mass widens steadily from crown to
   shoulders. Remove the waist and the V notch at the back. Lengthen the outer fringe to the lash line with thin,
   uneven tips. Flatten and taper the chest locks so they stop reading as cords. Vary the hanging ends. Remove
   the stray wires at the chin. **Worth a round** (it can share one with item 1).
3. **FACE: the nose highlight.** Make it a soft-edged, warm, smaller glow with a small glint, and fade it out on
   the flank at 30–60°. `fva_front.jpg`, `ft30.jpg`, `ft60.jpg`. **Polish**, but it is the cheapest big win and
   the first thing seen.
4. **FACE: the lash mass and corners.** A brown, thicker, feathered upper lash mass that overhangs the top of the
   iris and fans past the outer corner, joined to the flick. The outer white tucked under the lid instead of the
   ragged vertical edge. The 60° inner-corner block tapered. `eye_front_b2d.jpg`, `eye_60_b2d.jpg` against
   `eye_front_art.jpg`. **Worth a round** (a face-drawing pass).
5. **LOOK: latex sheen with form.** Brighter (90th percentile toward 190), larger, placed on the bust's upper
   outer curves, shoulders and upper arms, with the upper chest and underbust back to dark. The flank and arm
   stripes broken. `pvc_torso.jpg`. **Worth a round** (lookdev).
6. **OTHER: pose the head** in the concept framing: tilt, chin down, eyes up to camera. **Polish.**
7. **HAIR: the ear at 60–90°.** Let a lock cover the ear's top, or round the cap's cut, so the visible ear is
   round. `ft90.jpg`, `pf_sidehead.jpg`. **Polish.**
8. **LOOK: shadows.** Crisp, one-step fringe and side-lock shadows instead of blurred streaks on the forehead
   and near cheek. A deeper under-chin toward (175, 125, 110). **Polish.**
9. **FACE: blush down about a fifth, skin a touch warmer and darker.** `pvc_face2.jpg`. **Polish.**
10. **LOOK: knee pads.** Give the pad's body the suit's black and gloss, keeping the new dome cap. `knee.jpg`.
    **Polish.** (Also: the toon panel of `posed_hotlight.jpg` is still labelled "B2b".)

## 7. Verdict

**Is she ready to show the owner as "close to the picture"? Close in the face, not yet in the hair. Not yet as
a whole.** B2c and B2d did real work. The hair frames the face at the drawing's widths, the eyes have weight,
the brows give her the serious look, the mouth and blush are the drawing's, and the chest blobs are gone. In
close-ups of the eyes and face (`eyes_b2d.jpg`, `ft30.jpg`) she now looks like the same character.

**What he will notice first:**
- **The hair is one flat dark brown, shaped like a hood**, where the picture's hair is lit, ash-toned and
  parted (`fva_front.jpg`, `posed_vs_concept.jpg`).
- **The white sticker on the nose**, from the front and worse at three-quarter (`ft30.jpg`).
- **She stands straight and looks ahead**, where the picture tilts her head and lowers her chin.

One hair round (items 1–2), with items 3 and 6 folded in, should bring her to "close". The lash mass and the
latex sheen would then be what separates her from "as good as the picture".
