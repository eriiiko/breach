2026-10-08. I judged by eye from the pictures in `previews/`. The B1i set came from git (`1877dc8`) for
before/after. I read no builder notes. I opened every picture named below whole, and cropped, paired
old/new, or contrast-stretched the places in question (crops in `C:/tmp/beatrice_rev10/v/`; `p_<name>.jpg`
is B1i on the left and now on the right, `z_*.jpg` are zoomed pairs). References: `without_hair.png`,
`_side.png`, `_back.png`, `concept.jfif`. The head, the low heel and the owner's three open questions are
not judged.

Measurements: `sheet.png`, `side.png` and `back.png` share the drawings' 1536-px frame (1 px is about 1.2 mm).
I measured widths as runs of pixels that differ from the background (summed RGB difference > 40), every 20 rows,
on drawing, B1i and now. For the wrist I used a skin mask (red minus blue > 25), row by row below the cuff. For
tone I used luminance percentiles in fixed boxes on `sheet.png` and `without_hair.png`.

## 2. The hands, cuffs and knee pads at close-up quality

- **Hands:** at last they read as a slim woman's relaxed hands in `hand.jpg`, `wrist.jpg` and the matte views.
  Proportions are right, the knuckle transition is natural, and the nails are good. Remaining, in order:
  (a) from straight front, the thumb-and-fingers pincer with an empty V between them (`hands_sheet.jpg`);
  (b) the thumb is a plain tube with no thenar pad flowing into it on the palm side (`p_hand_palm.jpg`);
  (c) a short crease line where the thumb's base meets the wrist, seen in the back ortho
  (`hands_b1j.jpg` back B1j). They are polish, not a round.
- **Cuffs:** seated, a lower lip that rolls in, the wrist right. They still read as a stiff ring rather than a
  turned-back glossy band, and the face has the soft dent. Polish.
- **Knee pads:** see item 8. This is the one leg element still drawing the eye at full-figure distance.

## 5. The whole figure, now

She now reads as one coherent, clean character in a glossy suit. No part looks like an add-on except the

design density**, not anatomy.

**Ranked, what most separates her from finished (head and hair excluded):**

1. **Back-armpit knot.** SURFACE/DETAIL. **Worth a round** (short, but must happen before showing).
   `z_ab.jpg`, `z_sb.jpg`, visible in `hero_back.jpg` (`z_hb_arm.jpg`). The drawing (`without_hair_back.png`)

6. **Arms thin and long.** SHAPE. **Polish.** Forearm −13%, upper arm −8%, hands about 1.8 cm low

8. **Hands from straight front: thumb/finger pincer, tube thumb.** SHAPE. **Polish.** `hands_sheet.jpg`,

## 6. Verdict

**Is the body (without head) ready to show the owner as a complete pass? Almost. One short fix first.**
B1j and B1k did what was asked. The hands are now acceptable at close-up. The shoulder ripples, the mid-arm
garter and the collar ledge are gone, and the mesh has the right colour. But B1k left a **visible break at both
back armpits**, worse than the tear it replaced and visible in `hero_back.jpg`. A "complete pass" should not
carry a geometry knot. Fix item 1 (and ideally item 2, the pad dome, a small job). Then show her.

**What he will notice first:**
- `hero.jpg` / `legs.jpg`: the knee pads as grey porthole lenses. They are the lightest shapes below the hips.
- `hero_back.jpg`: the dark knots in both armpits.
- Stepping back, beside the drawing: the suit reads as satin vinyl with thin seams rather than the drawing's
  wet latex with bold piping and big front mesh panels. The figure is slimmer at the hips (his open question).
