# Beatrice, after the pelvis round — review

2026-10-04. The round reworked the pelvis only (waist, hips, seat, upper thighs), because the owner
makes his go/no-go on the character from the hips: he wants the "one step slimmer" shape and no
"sharp edges on her hips". An Opus reviewer examined `hips.jpg`, `hips_vs_drawings.jpg`,
`hips_before_after.jpg`, `shape_vs_concept.jpg` and the glossy set against the drawings, without
seeing the builder's report. (`concept.jfif` did not open in the reviewer's tool; the concept was
judged from its crop inside the comparison pictures.)

## The corner (reviewer)
- **Front and back:** the hard corner at crotch height is gone on both sides, matte and glossy.
  What remains in the front view is a soft, short bend just above crotch height, where a nearly
  straight diagonal from the waist turns into a nearly upright thigh. It does not read as an edge.
- **Side, underside of the seat** (`hips.jpg` side panel, just below crotch height): the outline
  leaves the seat bulge and turns back into the thigh abruptly — a concave notch, the sharpest turn
  left in the region.
- **Three-quarter back, outer outline:** no corner, but almost straight from waist to knee.
- **Three-quarter front:** no corner.

## The curve (reviewer)
- **Front:** still more cone than S-curve: the waist pinch did not change, so the reverse curve that
  should feed the hip is weak; below the bend the thigh tapers almost straight to the knee.
- **Back:** a gentle, pleasant convex line from waist over hip into thigh.
- **Side — the weakest view:** the lower back is almost straight down to the seat; the seat is a
  short, low bump that starts late and ends at the notch; the back of the thigh is straight below
  it; the front of the thigh is a dead straight vertical from belly to knee. It reads as a tube
  with a small seat added.

## The size (reviewer)
About right for the slim build: the model sits clearly inside the drawings' outline at the hips.
Against the concept crop the hip width matches, but the concept's waist is narrower relative to the
hips and its upper thighs are fuller. The model reads "too straight" rather than "too wide". Not to
be made slimmer.

## Other defects in the pelvis (reviewer, most important first)
1. The gap between the legs: a narrow pointed arch with a dark crease, and the inner thighs fall
   away at once, so the legs read as a V. The drawing's inner thighs nearly touch for a while.
2. The seat's lower edge: almost no fold under the buttocks; in the three-quarter back view the
   cleft is a long, hard, dark groove, and there is a dark dent at the back of the waist.
3. Faint shading bands in matte grey: a horizontal ring across both thighs at crotch height, faint
   vertical streaks on the front of the thighs. Not visible in the glossy renders.
4. The outer-thigh mesh panel sits just inside the outline and stiffens the glossy hip silhouette.

**Reviewer's verdict:** accept with reservations, in front and back view only; the side profile
would be rejected as it stands.

## The builder's own list (from its report)
Agrees with the reviewer on every point it names: the pointed gap (rounding it in the loft bulged
the three-quarter outlines; a proper fix is an implicit surface for the crotch region, like the
hands and boots), the seat reading as a bump on a straight thigh, the front of the thigh nearly
flat (about 1 mm of swell), the waist-to-hip run close to straight between 1.05 and 0.93 m, and a
dark patch at the lower back in the three-quarter back view.

Measured: worst outline turn in the pelvis, front/back, 2.6 deg/cm (was 12.7); side front 3.2,
side back 4.4. Hip/waist ratio 1.795 against the concept's 1.794.

## Orchestrator
Looked at the four hip pictures. Agrees: the corner the owner named is gone in front and back; the
back view is good; the side view is the weak one (seat as a bump with a notch under it, straight
front thigh, straight lower back); the leg gap is pointed. The numbers say "no corner"; they do
not say "natural" — the side view shows that.

Decision: nothing further is built until the owner has looked at `hips.jpg` and said go, one more
round on the side profile, or pause.
