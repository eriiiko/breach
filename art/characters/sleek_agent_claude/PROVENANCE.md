## Reproduce

Blender 4.5.10 LTS, bundled Python. From `art/characters/`:

    bash charkit/run.sh sleek_agent_claude --turn --beauty all --samples 128 --save   # about 2 minutes
    bash charkit/run.sh sleek_agent_claude --draft --turn                            # about 12 seconds

Outputs: `previews/` (tracked), `source/sleek_agent.blend` (gitignored, regenerated by
`--save`) and `source/mesh_stats.json` (tracked). The modelling kit, studio and build driver
are the shared `art/characters/charkit/`.

## What is modelled

1.74 m to the helmet top, slim athletic build, neutral A-pose, empty relaxed hands.

- Bodysuit: ONE welded surface from ankle to neck (each leg's section grows into half the
  pelvis, then half the torso), charcoal, with bonded seam tape, stitched seams and ribbed
  stretch panels at the neck, waist sides, inner elbow and knee hollow. Sleeves separate.
- Armour (pale, faceted, chamfered): breastplate, back plate with power cell, segmented spine
  guard, pauldrons, bracers, elbow caps, pointed-knee greaves with hinge discs, boot plates,
  glove plates, and the artwork's one-sided collar plate.
- Helmet: domed shell with a wrap-round black visor, respirator with filter caps, jaw guards,
  ear modules (a comms stub on the right one), neck seal.
- Gear: shoulder and rib harness with bronze buckles, underbust strap, belt with four pouches,
  an asymmetric thigh rig (two straps and a pouch on the right leg, one and a small pouch on
  the left), an upper-arm patch on the right sleeve.

## Measured

`source/mesh_stats.json`: 89 mesh objects, 411,190 source triangles, 896,658 after modifiers
(suit 224 k, helmet 75 k, boots 73 k, gloves 27 k, gear 6 k, armour 6 k source).
No silhouette score: a single perspective view of a posed figure with props is not a
measurable target the way the marine's orthographic sheet was. The gallery shows the artwork
beside a render from a similar low camera for a judgement by eye.

## Not done, and known weaknesses

- No UV maps, no bake, no rig, no skin weights, no export, no engine test.
- A high-resolution source, not a game mesh. Parts overlap rather than weld, except the suit.
- Against the artwork: the proportions are a realistic slim build, not the artwork's
  stretched legs; the armour has fewer bolts, slots and scuffs; the suit's fine hex-mesh
  texture is plain ribbing; the visor is a simpler band. The gripping hands of the artwork
  are replaced by relaxed ones, as there are no props.
- Materials are Cycles node graphs and do not carry to the game's shader.
