## How it is built

She is the male worker's builder with her own tables; nothing of the builder is copied here.

- `scripts/worker.py` — the tables, the same keys as the male's: `PALETTE` (the only place a
  colour lives; the same named materials `worker_skin`, `worker_hair`, `worker_eye`,
  `worker_coverall`, `worker_undershirt`, `worker_boot`, `worker_sole`, `worker_lace`,
  `worker_metal`), `DIRT`, `VARIANTS`, `DIMS`, `HEAD`, `HAIR`, `BEAUTY`.
- `scripts/sheet_ref.py` — her sheet's pixel frame (soles on row 950, hair top row 15, panel
  columns 321 / 760 / 1215; the side view faces left).
- `scripts/build.py` — three lines over `charkit/workerbuild.py`, the build driver both workers share.
- What the shared kit gained for her (additive; the male builds as before): asymmetric chest
  pockets (`chest_pocket` `left="flap"`, `right="zip"`: a buttoned flap with a pen slot on one,
  a zip on the other), the sleeve pocket optional, belt loops (`belt_loops`), back darts and a
  centre-back seam (`back_darts`, `back_centre`), the slanted front hip-pocket openings
  (`hip_pocket`), a plain waistband (`elastic=0`), cargo snaps optional, chevron hems on
  patches (`garment.patch(point=)`, the back pockets), the brow and stubble specs of
  `parts.face_field` (`HEAD["brow"]`, `HEAD["stubble"]`), hair drawn back to a point
  (`HAIR["flow_to"]`), `parts.hair_bun` (a coiled bun, one closed object) and
  `parts.hair_strands` (loose strands at the temples, capped tubes).

## Not done, and known weaknesses

- No UV maps, no bake, no rig, no game mesh. Pieces overlap rather than weld, except the coverall's
  two halves.
- The face is the weakest part: the male's displacement face with her own spec (narrower jaw, no
  stubble, finer arched brows, fuller lips, a smaller nose). It reads as a stylised mannequin, not
  plainly as a woman's face; the silhouette carries her build, the face does not.
- The hair is a shell: the pulled-back hair reads as a smooth cap with a hard hairline; the bun is a
  coiled ellipsoid; the loose strands are thin stiff tubes and do not yet look like hair.
- The bust lives in the chest rows' depth only (a front-to-back bulge), so from the front it is
  soft; a displacement bump was tried and dropped because patches (the chest pockets) sit on the
  undisplaced surface and were swallowed by it.
- The back darts are straight lines; the artwork's curve towards the side seam. The hip-pocket
  openings are stitched lines without an opening in the cloth.
- The waistband stands proud like a belt; the boots are the male's boot shape scaled down.
- Materials are Cycles node graphs; the dirt and region masks will need baking for the game mesh.
