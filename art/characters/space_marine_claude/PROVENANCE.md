## How it is built

- `../charkit/kit.py` — the modelling kit, shared with the other characters. A `Loft` is a tube of superellipse sections along a
  centre line, kept as an analytic surface `(phi, t) -> point, normal`. Armour plates, straps,
  pockets and pouches are built in a metric frame anchored ON those surfaces (`OnLoft`,
  `OnEllipsoid`, `OnPlane`), so they follow the body they sit on. Cloth folds are authored
  ridges displaced along the loft normal; `flex` and `seam` are per-vertex attributes the
  materials read (black ribbed stretch panels, stitched seam lines).
- `scripts/marine.py` — the character: every dimension in metres, fitted to the artwork.
  Limbs are modelled on the left and mirrored. The trousers are one welded surface with a
  real centre seam (each leg's section grows into half the pelvis and is clamped at the mid-plane).
- `scripts/scene.py` — the marine's materials, the orthographic re-shoot of the sheet and the
  silhouette comparison; studio rig, cameras and material builders are `../charkit/studio.py`.
- `scripts/refkit.py` — the reference sheet's pixel frame and its figure mask.

## Game mesh (static, unrigged)

2026-10-04, tracker #33 P1. One command, scripts to outputs, about 85 s on an RTX 3070:

    cd art/characters/space_marine_claude
    "C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 4.5/blender.exe" -b --factory-startup -P scripts/game.py -- [--tris 10000]

`scripts/game.py` is the ten-line entry; the method is the shared `../charkit/gameready.py`
(options in its docstring). Outputs: `game/space_marine.glb`, `game/albedo.png`, `game/normal.png`,
`game/space_marine_game.blend` (all gitignored, regenerated), `game/stats.json` (tracked), and
`previews/game_turnaround.png`, `previews/game_top.jpg`, `previews/game_vs_source.jpg`.

Method: the full-resolution build is the bake source. A copy is applied and joined; the 28 open
cloth sheets are first given a wall 1.5 voxels thick grown inward (a sheet thinner than a voxel
falls apart in the level set); a 6 mm voxel remesh makes it one surface; the body's hollow inside
(which stays connected to the outside through tube ends) is closed in voxel space -- a cell is
inside unless it sees the grid border along one of the six axes past the solid dilated by two
voxels -- stuffed with a block filler and remeshed again, leaving one outer surface; enclosed
shells and crumbs are dropped; an X-symmetric collapse decimate takes it to 10,000 triangles,
smooth-shaded, no split normals. UVs: Smart UV Project (551 islands on this surface), every island
under 24 faces merged into its longest-border neighbour, charts flattened angle-based; a chart that
folds, crushes part of itself or overlaps itself is re-cut by Smart UV at half the angle (66, 33,
16.5 deg) and finally left as its bare Smart UV islands. Bakes (Cycles, OptiX, selected-to-active,
cage extrusion 12 mm, max ray 40 mm, 128 samples, 16 px margin) come from ONE joined copy of the
source -- Cycles re-syncs the whole scene per selected object, so 105 objects made each bake take
minutes instead of seconds -- in which closed parts whose faces pointed inward are turned outward
(Pouch_Front, Pouch_Front_Flap, Boot_Ankle_Buckle, Boot_Heel: invisible in a render, they baked
black occlusion); normal map first with the original materials (shader bump included), then AO,
then every material re-wired to emit its own Base Color signal. Texels whose ray found nothing are
inpainted from their neighbours. `albedo.png` = base colour x AO, sRGB; `normal.png` tangent space,
OpenGL (+Y), linear. glTF: Y-up, metres, one mesh, one material, feet on the ground at the origin
(an offset of 1.0 / 2.0 / 0.0 cm is applied to the export only), facing glTF +Z (the source's -Y).

Measured (`game/stats.json`, run of 2026-10-04):

  and 0 non-manifold edges (watertight); 157 UV islands, all UVs inside [0.006, 0.994];
  0.57 % of the surface area sees no sky in 14 directions (deep creases, no hidden shells).
- Silhouette IoU of the game mesh against the source, orthographic: front 0.985, side 0.991,
  back 0.985 (front and back are mirror images: the model is symmetric). The loss is not
  itemised -- no difference image was rendered; the turnaround shows hoses, handle, straps and
  buckles present, their edges rounded by the 6 mm voxels.
- Bake: 41 % of the texture is covered by charts; 0.52 % of covered texels missed (inpainted);
  135 texels (0.03 %) covered twice by one chart that still overlaps itself.
- raylib 5.5 (pyray `load_model`): 1 mesh, vertexCount 7,029, triangleCount 9,999, tangents present,
  albedo and normal textures loaded (1024 px). The game's current marine
  (`assets/models/marine/AnimationLibrary_Godot_Standard.gltf`): 2 meshes, 8,547 vertices, 13,743
  triangles.

Known weaknesses of the game mesh:

- Flat panels (the pack's back face above all) come out of the collapse decimate dented, with
  sliver triangles; the baked normal map flattens their shading, but in a Cycles preview the real
  dents still self-shadow in wedge shapes. A rasterizer without shadow rays should not show them;
  not yet checked in the game's own shader.
- The visor loses its gloss: the export carries base colour and normals only (roughness is a flat
  0.6, no metal, no coat).
- Texture use is 41 %: the charts are irregular; texel density varies about +/-20 % (5th-95th pct).
- The exporter drops one of the 10,000 triangles (raylib counts 9,999) -- presumably a degenerate one.
- No rig, no skin weights, no LODs; the game's marine shader has not been tried on it.

## Not done, and known weaknesses (of the source model)

- No rig, no skin weights, no engine test. (UVs, texture bake and a static glTF export: see Game mesh.) The
  Quaternius 53-bone skeleton (`assets/models/marine/`) is untouched and nothing is bound to it.
- This is a high-resolution source, not a game mesh: a 4 mm grid on the suit. A game asset needs
  retopology or decimation and a normal-map bake of the folds and plate edges.
- Parts overlap rather than weld (except the trousers); loft ends are open where a neighbour
  hides them. Fine for rendering, not for deformation.
- The fine wrinkling is half geometry (authored folds), half shader bump; the bump does not export.
- Materials are Cycles node graphs (ambient-occlusion and bevel nodes): they do not carry to
  the game's `marine_shader.py`.
- Against the artwork: the collar mantle and knee pads are rounder than the artwork's faceted
  plates; the boots have fewer layered panels; the cloth is less heavily crumpled; the shin's
  side zip and the small helmet vents are missing. The model is exactly left-right symmetric.
