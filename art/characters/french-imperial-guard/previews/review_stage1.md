# Review, stage 1: French Imperial Guard officer (independent, by eye)

Reviewed 2026-10-05 from the renders in `previews/` and the two references only:
no builder notes, scripts or history were read. "His left / his right" mean the
character's own sides. Viewing copies and zoomed crops: `C:/tmp/guard_review/`.

## 1. Verdict

From the front he reads as a hussar officer straight away. The outline matches the
sheet very closely in all three views, and the colours, chest braid, cross-belt, sash,
breeches knots and boots are all there. He stops reading as the drawn officer as soon
as the pelisse comes into view: it is a stiff, box-shaped shell standing off his left
shoulder and arm. Together with the spiky "starburst" plumes and a peak that wraps round
the shako like a top-hat brim, it is the strongest impression from the side, the back,
the elevated view and the game camera. Those three items, and none of the many small
details, are what keep this from being a good match.

## 2. Defects (worst first)

1. **The pelisse is a rigid shell, not a slung garment.** SHAPE.
   *Where:* `torso_front.jpg` (right half), `torso_back.jpg`, `pelisse.jpg`, `elevated.jpg`, `sheet_vs_reference.png` side and back panels, the blue block in the back view of `sheet_overlay.png`.
   *Model:* a flat, horizontal fur shelf sits on top of his left shoulder. From it a navy panel drops straight down like the wall of a drum, standing several centimetres out from the left upper arm. It ends in a hard horizontal fur hem at waist height, with square corners. The upper arm is fully enclosed, and the forearm comes out under the hem as if leaving a box. From the back, the panel is one flat navy rectangle covering the whole left half of his back, reaching past the body's outline on that side. From the side, the whole upper body reads as a cube with fur on every edge, and the chest braid is hidden.
   *Sheet:* the pelisse lies on the body. Its front panel lies flat over the left chest, with its own frogging. The fur front edge runs from the neck **diagonally** down and outward to the left hip, and the hem fur rises toward the left elbow. In front, the left upper arm is visible as a navy dolman sleeve. From the back, the readable shapes are a thick fur band running diagonally from the neck to the waist, a fur hem across the waist, and the dolman's back braid still visible on both halves. From the side, the braided chest shows and the pelisse sits on the back. The fur collar wraps round the back and side of the neck (red above the shoulder in all three overlay views), where the model has a flat ledge.

2. **The plumes are spiky shards, not feathers.** SURFACE and SHAPE.
   *Where:* `head.jpg`, `head_side.jpg`, `elev_head`, `top.jpg` (most visible here).
   *Model:* each plume is a cluster of large, hard, leaf-like spikes, each about a sixth of the plume's width, with jagged tips. It reads as a dark pine cone, a succulent or a sea urchin. From straight above, the black plume is an eight-pointed black star and the red one a red starburst. The outline matches the sheet's in size (overlay), but its edge is jagged everywhere (the blue spikes in the overlay).
   *Sheet:* tall, dense, soft feather plumes. Each is a smooth tapered ovoid, narrow at the socket, with a fine hairy edge and no large spikes. The painting shows the same soft pile.

3. **The shako's peak wraps round like a top-hat brim.** SHAPE and SURFACE.
   *Where:* `head.jpg` (the brim runs past both sides of the shako body), `elev_head` (a wide black disc round the front half and both sides), `head_side.jpg`.
   *Model:* a thin, flat, matte, horizontal plate that reaches round to the ears. Together with the outward-flaring cylinder, the elevated and hero views read as a top hat.
   *Sheet/painting:* a peak at the front only, shaped, angled down over the eyes, glossy black leather.

4. **The pelisse's empty sleeve hangs in the wrong place.** SHAPE.
   *Where:* `torso_back.jpg` (a short navy tube with chevrons and a fur cuff, hanging from under the pelisse hem near the spine, above his left buttock), the red patch under the left arm in the back view of `sheet_overlay.png`, the red patch behind the hip in its side view.
   *Model:* the sleeve is a short stub, about forearm length, that starts at the hem rather than at the shoulder. From the front it is only a fur ring wedged between torso and arm.
   *Sheet:* the back view shows the sleeve hanging from the shoulder **outside** his left arm, with the gold chevrons and a large fur cuff ending level with and outside the hand. The side view shows it behind the arm at the hip, lower and further back than the model's.

5. **The fur is the wrong colour and reads as sponge.** SURFACE.
   *Where:* every fur edge, clearest in `torso_back.jpg`, `head_side.jpg` and `pelisse.jpg`.
   *Model:* a light greyish taupe (about RGB 123/103/86 in `hero.jpg`). The surface is lumpy and crumbly, like cork or sponge, and its edges are jagged lumps rather than soft pile.
   *Sheet:* a medium olive-brown (about 98/86/63) with soft, directional pile. *Painting:* a deep chocolate mink (about 40/31/18). The model's fur is far too light and too grey, and has no pile direction.

6. **The boots are a pipe stuck into a clog.** SHAPE and SURFACE.
   *Where:* `boots.jpg`, `elev_legs`, side panel of `sheet_vs_reference.png`.
   *Model:* a straight cylindrical shaft with no taper at the ankle, sitting in a separate rounded "slipper" foot, with a visible seam and ring where they meet. The foot is a blunt duck-bill shape on a thin wedge, and the whole boot has a uniform chrome-mirror gloss that reads as metal or plastic. From the front the feet are a little narrower than the drawing's (red edge round both feet in the front overlay).
   *Sheet:* one leather boot. The shaft tapers to the ankle with creases there, and the boot has a defined heel, a slimmer, rounded and slightly longer toe, and a spur strap across the instep. The painting confirms a high polish but with leather highlights, not a mirror.

7. **The boot top has no scallop, and red cloth shows through its notch.** DETAIL.
   *Where:* `boots.jpg`, `pairL_front`.
   *Model:* a straight top edge with a thin gold line and a small V cut at the front. Red breeches fill the V down to below the trim, and a small tassel hangs in it.
   *Sheet:* a wide gold edge forming a heart-shaped scallop (two arcs meeting in a point at the front centre), black leather inside it, and the tassel hanging from the point.

8. **The sash is salmon pink, not crimson.** SURFACE.
   *Where:* `torso_front.jpg`, `hero.jpg`.
   *Model:* pinkish salmon (about 178/103/85) with thin horizontal stripes.
   *Sheet:* a dark crimson-maroon (about 89/60/56) with gold barrels and a gold central plate. The painting is darker still. Against the scarlet breeches the model's sash is the same family of colour, but lighter, and does not separate from them as the sheet's does.

9. **The shako's front plate and cords are too simple.** DETAIL.
   *Where:* `head.jpg`.
   *Model:* the plate is a plain gold domed oval that reads as a gold egg. There is one twisted cord swag across the front, and the chin strap is a plain flat gold band with no bosses.
   *Sheet:* an eagle on a shaped plate under the cockade; two cord festoons (a high one round the plate and a heavy lower swag) with flounders; chinscales hung from round bosses at the sides (clear in the side view).

10. **The cuffs are straight-cut, and the chevrons are short dashes.** DETAIL and SHAPE.
    *Where:* `torso_front.jpg` (both forearms), `hero.jpg`.
    *Model:* a straight red band with a gold top line. Above it, two or three thin diagonal gold strokes on his right, and dotted dashes on his left.
    *Sheet:* a pointed red cuff with gold edging, and gold chevrons (a V knot) on the lower sleeve above it. These chevrons are one of the strongest details in all three views of the sheet.

11. **The back braid is incomplete, and the shoulder cords are missing.** DETAIL.
    *Where:* `torso_back.jpg`.
    *Model:* one back-seam line with a single trefoil, on his right half only. The left half is covered by the pelisse panel (see 1). There are no gold shoulder cords.
    *Sheet:* two curved side-back seams, each with a trefoil top and bottom, a centre-back seam ending in a trefoil, braid along the dolman's lower edge, and a gold shoulder cord running from the collar to the shoulder point (back view: his right shoulder; side view: his left).

12. **The pelisse cord across the chest is missing.** DETAIL.

14. **The breeches knots are thin single outlines.** DETAIL.
    *Where:* `pairL_front`, `torso_front.jpg`.

    *Sheet:* symmetric seat piping curving from the waist over each buttock into the outer-seam stripes. The stripes show at the leg's edge in both the front and back views, and two small knots sit at the back waist.

16. **The hair is wrong in three places: no sideburns, a hard nape patch, protruding ears.** SURFACE.

## 4. From the game camera (`top.jpg`, `elevated.jpg`)

**What reads:**
- The two plumes, a large black one and a red one.
- The shako's black crown, a big disc with a thin gold rim.
- A brown fur patch on his left shoulder.
- Navy shoulders, with a red cuff and white glove at the end of the right arm.

He is clearly "a soldier with a plumed tall hat".

**What does not read:**
- The plumes read as two spiky starbursts: the black one as an eight-pointed black star overhanging the front rim, the red one as a red burst over the fur. They look like sea urchins or flowers, not feathers.
- The fur patch is a flat tan oval, the top of the pelisse shelf. It reads as a pad, not a slung fur garment, and is barely darker than the background grey.
- The gold rim on the crown is a thin line; the sheet's wide gold top band would be a strong gold ring from above.
- The red breeches, the gold chest braid and the sash are invisible from straight above, so the red-and-gold scheme is lost and he reads as almost all dark.
- In the elevated view the wrap-round brim makes the shako a top hat.
- The pelisse makes the left shoulder a hunched hump, as wide again as the right one.

**What would make him recognisable at a glance:**
1. Soft feather plumes, so that from above they are round, fuzzy tufts in black and red rather than stars.
2. A darker, richer brown fur lying diagonally across the shoulder and upper back, as the sheet's back view shows. A dark diagonal stripe from the neck across the back is a distinctive top-down mark.
3. A visibly wider gold top band on the crown, so a gold ring frames the plumes.
4. A front-only, downward-angled peak, giving a short dark tab at the front of the disc from above, which also shows which way he faces.
5. A narrower left shoulder once the pelisse lies on the body, so the shoulder line reads symmetric apart from the fur.

## 5. What is good: do not change

1. **The outline and proportions.** The model matches the sheet's front, side and back outlines almost pixel for pixel: head, shako height and width, shoulders, arm length, legs, boot height, foot length. Corrections must keep the overlay grey.
2. **The colour scheme.** Dark navy dolman, scarlet breeches, black boots, gold braid, white gloves, black-on-his-right and red-on-his-left plumes. The navy is right (decision 4).
3. **The front of the dolman as a composition.** Chest braid rows with loop ends, the cross-belt correctly from his left shoulder to his right hip with its badge, the barrel sash's gold barrels and plate, the gold lower edge. From the front he is unmistakably a hussar.
4. **The shako's base.** Body proportion, gold top band, cockade, the cord and tassel down his right side, and the plumes' size and placement (only their surface needs replacing).
5. **The face.** Doll-like but intact and pleasant, with the moustache's shape and colour right. Leave it.
6. **The breeches layout and boot fittings.** The knots are on the right place on the thighs, the side double stripe is right, and the boots have their front tassel and spurs. The fixes for these are detail changes, not rebuilds.

## 6. The five corrections I would order (one round)

1. **Re-hang the pelisse as cloth lying on the body.** Make it a thin garment that conforms to the left shoulder, upper arm and back, with no stand-off and no horizontal shelf. Wrap the fur collar round the back and side of the neck. Run the front-edge fur diagonally from the neck down and outward to the left hip, and let the fur hem rise toward the left elbow, with soft corners. Leave the left dolman sleeve visible from the front. Hang the empty sleeve **from the shoulder**, behind and outside the left arm, with its chevrons and a fur cuff ending outside the hand at wrist level (the sheet's back view). From the back, the reading should be the diagonal fur band plus the hem; show the dolman's back braid on both halves wherever the pelisse does not cover it, and add the missing centre seam, trefoils and shoulder cords there.
2. **Rebuild both plumes as soft feather pile.** Make each a smooth, tapered ovoid, narrow at the socket, with a fine hairy edge and no spikes wider than a few millimetres. Keep the current size and placement. Do the same for the green variant's single plume, with a red tip that blends in.
3. **Make the shako peak front-only.** Shape it, angle it down over the eyes and make it glossy black leather. Stop it at the cockade-to-ear line, with nothing round the sides. In the same pass, replace the egg plate with an eagle on a shaped plate, add the second cord festoon, and hang the chinscales from round side bosses.
4. **Rebuild the boots as one leather piece.** Taper the shaft into the ankle with creases, then a slimmer rounded toe, a real heel and the spur strap across the instep, with no seam or ring between shaft and foot. Lower the gloss from chrome to polished leather. Give the top the sheet's wide gold heart-shaped scallop with black leather inside and the tassel at its point, and no red cloth in the notch.
5. **Fix the fur and the sash.** Darken the fur to a rich medium-to-dark brown, between the sheet and the painting, and give it soft, directional pile with soft edges instead of sponge lumps. Turn the sash to a dark crimson-maroon so it separates from the scarlet breeches. If any budget remains, cut pointed cuffs with the gold V chevron above them, since that detail appears in all three views of the sheet.
