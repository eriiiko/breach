Officer of the Imperial Guard light cavalry — scripted Blender model

Built from Python alone on the shared character kit (scripts/build.py, Blender 4.5, Cycles), from a palette, a dimensions table, head / hair / plume specs in scripts/guard.py and the assembly in scripts/assemble.py. Character only: no saber, scabbard, slings or sabretache. See PROVENANCE.md.

Correction round: stage 1 against now

The one correction round after the stage-1 review (review_stage1.md): the pelisse rebuilt to lie on the body (its surface carried onto the dolman's own trunk and sleeve), soft feather plumes, a front-only peak, boots, fur, sash, cuffs and chevrons, then the shako's ornament, the back and chest cords, hair and the breeches' knots.

The sheet's figure mask

The sheet is drawn on a grid, inside a frame, with labels and a callout box whose leader line comes within a few pixels of the front figure, and white gloves close to the backdrop's colour. The figures are cut out once (scripts/make_mask.py): thin lines with backdrop on both sides removed, outlines closed, enclosed gaps filled, the callout and frame blanked (red boxes).
