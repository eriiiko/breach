# Golden re-baseline — velocity-clamp arc close (2026-08-20)

Written rationale for the ONE deliberate golden re-baseline this arc is
allowed, per Erik's ruling (2026-08-19, recorded in `docs/TODO.md`): the
canon-fold cadence is deferred while the physics side keeps gaining whole
systems, but accumulated re-baseline debt still gets settled once, at arc
close, with a written record. This is that record.

## Why now, and what it settles

Two independent buckets of stale/absent goldens had accumulated since the
last committed baseline (`add8969`, Arc B, July):

1. **The 11 `GOLDEN_AGGREGATE`-dependent test files** — flipped red by this
   arc's own behavioral change (P-V1, the velocity clamp). Documented and
   predicted at patch time in
   `docs/archive/velocity_clamp_pv1_asbuilt_2026-08-19.md`'s close-out note:
   "at close, re-baseline `GOLDEN_AGGREGATE` in
   `tests/_xarch_perfield_digest.py` (one event, with this doc as the
   lineage entry) — this fixes all 11 flipped tests plus
   `test_cuda_p64_kick_compression.py` in one commit."
2. **The six standing digest reds** (`test_b1_signal_bus`, `test_b2_nodes`,
   `test_b5_airlock`, and the three `test_b6_logic_golden` tests) — stale
   since Arc B (`add8969`, July), never folded into any of the intervening
   arc closes. Settled here as accumulated debt, not caused by
   velocity-clamp.

## Approved behavioral changes folded into the `GOLDEN_AGGREGATE` re-baseline

The canonical A/B scenario (`field_ab_harness.default_scenario_sim`, 30
ticks) exercises the EOS, so every deliberate physics arc since `add8969`
lawfully moves its trajectory. Four approved changes are folded into this one
re-baseline event (none re-baselined individually at their own close, per the
deferred canon-fold cadence):

1. **energy-books arc** (closed 2026-08-17) — EOS moves energy, not copied
   T; `k_drag = 0.5` shipped.
2. **pressure arc** (closed 2026-08-18) — `mg_cycles` 2 → 8
   (`docs/pressure_arc_root_cause_2026-08-17.md`).
3. **mass-books arc** (closed 2026-08-19, `cd83d1a`) — `destroy_wall`
   constant ambient seed.
4. **velocity-clamp arc** (this close, HUMAN-TEST PASS
   `docs/human_test_2026-08-20_velocity_clamp.md`) — the global-scalar
   velocity cap replaced by a per-cell cap² plane, plus the Chebyshev-pretest
   diagonal-leak fix replaced by an exact squared-magnitude clamp
   (`docs/archive/velocity_clamp_pv1_asbuilt_2026-08-19.md` sites 1-18).

Items 1-3 were already latent in the pre-arc tree's un-rebased trajectory
(the golden had been stale since `add8969` without anyone folding it at
those arcs' closes); item 4 is the incremental move measured at P-V1 gate 5.
`DIGEST_SPEC_VERSION` is unchanged for all four — values moved, no field was
added/removed/retyped.
