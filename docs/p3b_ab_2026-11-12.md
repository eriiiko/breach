# P3b A/B — Philox at the draw sites, before vs after (2026-11-12)

#29 P3b (`docs/residency_batching_design_2026-11-07.md` §4.7, §9) changed
WHICH random numbers the sim draws: every draw is one keyed Philox-4x32-10
block (`simulation/draws.py`, key = the seed, counter = (tick, index, salt,
id)) where P3e drew the next word of one numpy stream. The word-to-outcome
maps are P3e's, unchanged. Sites: the shot and beam cones, the exposure and
crit rolls, the order path's melee crit, and the FieldEdit noise (on every
path).

**Before** = main `321babcf` (a detached checkout with the worktree's
compiled module; its numbers reproduce P3e's "after" column, as they
should). **After** = branch `29-p3b-philox` at `3692297e`. s.e. = standard
error of the mean over team-matches; "diff / s.e." is the difference over
the combined s.e. (|x| < 2 is inside seed noise).

## 0. Sole cause (before any number below)

`tests/_p3b_sole_cause.py`, a pytest plugin, replaces every keyed draw's word
by the word P3e drew at that point (the next raw step of
`np.random.default_rng(seed)`, one stream per sim, in call order). Run on the
branch against the eight pinned matches AT MAIN'S PINS:

    <breach py> -m pytest -p _p3b_sole_cause <the eight pinned tests>   (PYTHONPATH tests;src)

8 passed — outcome, ticks, field digest, tick digest and unit stream of
every pinned match come back bit for bit. So the draw sites, their order
and their maps are unchanged; the generator is the whole move. A scene that
draws nothing does not move at all: `GOLDEN_AGGREGATE` is unmoved
(`test_canonical_scenario_consumes_no_rng`: zero words drawn).
