## 1. WoW: The Burning Crusade (2.0–2.4)

### Quick corrections to your memory
- **Rogue** "4% physical-damage-taken" debuff (**Savage Combat**) is a **Wrath** talent — it did **not** exist in TBC. TBC rogue raid contribution = **Improved Expose Armor** (armor shred). You were right about the rest: Sap/Blind for "the CC zap," Rupture/Garrote/poisons for the bleeds.
- **Hunter's Mark** boosts **ranged Attack Power** for everyone attacking the target → **physical, not magic.**
- **Shaman "ankh"** = **Reincarnation** (self-res). Also: **Bloodlust/Heroism was PARTY-only** in TBC (raid-wide came in Wrath) — that's why raids stacked one shaman per group.
- **Priest Shackle Undead** = undead-only CC (you remembered correctly).
- **Balance druid Improved Faerie Fire** in TBC gave **+melee/ranged hit (physical)**, not spell hit (the spell-hit version is a Wrath rework).
- **Demonology** had **no Demonic Pact** in TBC (Wrath); **Affliction** had **no Haunt** (Wrath).

### Healers — what stood out for each
- **Holy Priest** — the flexible "jack of all trades" raid healer. Circle of Healing (instant smart group heal) + Prayer of Mending (fire-and-forget bouncing heal) + Prayer of Healing.
  - *Buffs:* Fortitude (stamina), Shadow Protection, Power Word: Shield. *Debuffs:* negligible. *CC:* Shackle Undead, Psychic Scream (AoE fear), Mind Soothe. *Utility:* **Mass Dispel** (priest-unique AoE dispel), Dispel Magic, Fear Ward, Spirit of Redemption. No battle-res / Bloodlust / Innervate.
- **Discipline Priest** — secondary spec, brought for cooldowns, not throughput.
  - *Signature:* **Power Infusion** (+20% spell haste on one ally) + **Pain Suppression**. *Buff:* **Improved Divine Spirit** (Spirit → bonus spell power/healing). Same priest CC/Mass Dispel/Fear Ward.
- **Restoration Druid** — the HoT specialist; most mana-efficient. Rolls Lifebloom/Rejuv/Regrowth + Swiftmend; Nature's Swiftness→Healing Touch emergency; Tranquility AoE.
  - *Buffs:* Mark of the Wild (all stats); **Tree of Life aura** (+25% of Spirit as party healing received). *CC:* Hibernate (beasts/dragonkin), Cyclone, Roots. *Utility:* **Innervate** (mana CD), **Rebirth — the only in-combat battle-res**, Remove Curse, Cure Poison. Cannot dispel Magic/Disease.
- **Restoration Shaman** — the most in-demand raid healer. **Chain Heal** (best AoE heal) + Earth Shield on the tank.
  - *Buffs (totems, party-only):* **Bloodlust/Heroism** (+30% haste), Mana Spring, Healing Stream, Wrath of Air (caster groups), Strength of Earth / Grace of Air / Windfury. *Utility:* **Mana Tide Totem** (party mana), **Tremor Totem** (anti-fear), Grounding Totem, Cleanse poison/disease, Purge, **Reincarnation (the ankh)**. No Innervate; cannot dispel Magic. *CC:* none hard (Frost Shock / Earthbind slows).
- **Holy Paladin** — premier single-target **tank** healer, most mana-durable (Illumination). Holy Light + Flash of Light. **No AoE heal, and no Beacon of Light (that's Wrath)** — glued to one tank.
  - *Buffs:* **Blessings** (Kings +10% stats, Wisdom mp5, Might AP, Salvation −30% threat, Light, Sanctuary) + **Auras** (Devotion armor, Concentration, Resistances). *Debuffs:* Judgement of Light (attackers heal) / Wisdom (attackers gain mana). *Utility:* **Cleanse** (poison+disease+magic), Blessing of Protection/Freedom/Sacrifice, Divine Intervention, Lay on Hands, Hammer of Justice (stun).

### Physical DPS — playstyle + what they brought
- **Arms Warrior** — Mortal Strike rhythm (Rend / Slam-weave / Whirlwind). **Why brought: Blood Frenzy** (+4% physical damage taken on the target, raid-wide, one Arms covers it). Also Battle/Commanding Shout, Demoralizing Shout, Sunder Armor.
- **Fury Warrior** — top raid melee DPS; Bloodthirst / Whirlwind / Rampage. Brings the shout kit + **Sunder Armor** (armor debuff — *shares a slot with rogue Expose Armor; they don't stack*). No Blood Frenzy.
- **Combat Rogue** — the raid rogue. Sinister Strike + never drop Slice and Dice; Adrenaline Rush / Blade Flurry. **Raid debuff: Improved Expose Armor** (bigger armor shred than Sunder). *CC:* Sap, Blind, Kick, Gouge.
- **Assassination / Mutilate Rogue** — mostly PvP/burst; Mutilate + poisons + Rupture bleeds. Can hold the Expose Armor slot (weaker without the Improved talent). Same CC.
- **Subtlety Rogue** — PvP/utility; PvE niche = the **Hemorrhage** debuff (+physical damage taken). Same CC kit.
- **Feral (cat) Druid** — hardest melee rotation (energy / powershifting / Shred from behind). **Debuffs: Mangle** (+30% bleed damage taken — boosts every bleed in the raid; doesn't stack with warrior Trauma) + **Faerie Fire** (armor). *CC:* Cyclone; brings Rebirth + Innervate from the class.
- **Enhancement Shaman** — brought almost entirely for **totem-twisting**: **Windfury Totem** (chance for extra melee attacks — the melee-group magnet), **Strength of Earth**, **Grace of Air**. Stormstrike adds a nature-damage-taken debuff.
- **Retribution Paladin** — seal-twisting DPS (Crusader Strike / Judgement). **Sanctity Aura** (+10% Holy damage), Blessings (Might/Wisdom), Judgements (Wisdom=mana back, Light=heal, Crusader=+Holy damage taken).
- **Beast Mastery Hunter** — highest hunter DPS; Steady Shot weave + pet. **Ferocious Inspiration** (+3% party damage on pet crit; stacks per BM hunter). Brings **Hunter's Mark**.
- **Marksmanship Hunter** — the "awkward middle"; slotted mainly to guarantee **Trueshot Aura** (+10% melee & ranged AP, raid-wide). Hunter's Mark.
- **Survival Hunter** — near-mandatory for **Expose Weakness** (a ranged crit applies a raid buff = +AP equal to 25% of *the hunter's* Agility). **Wyvern Sting** (sleep CC); Freezing/Frost traps.

### Caster DPS — playstyle + what they brought
- **Shadow Priest** — DoT/filler, brought for **support**: **Misery** (+5% spell damage taken & +5% spell hit on the target), **Vampiric Touch** (returns mana to the party), **Vampiric Embrace** (returns healing to the party), Shadow Weaving (+10% shadow damage taken). *CC:* Silence, Psychic Scream, Shackle Undead, Mind Control.
- **Fire Mage** — dominant raid mage; Fireball + Ignite/Combustion. A "Scorch bot" keeps **Improved Scorch / Fire Vulnerability** (+15% fire damage taken at 5 stacks). *CC:* Polymorph, Counterspell.
- **Frost Mage** — weakest raid mage; Frostbolt; brings **Winter's Chill** (+frost-spell crit vs target — narrow value). Polymorph etc.
- **Arcane Mage** — top burst mage; Arcane Blast burn/conserve. **No unique raid debuff** — pure throughput; badly wants a Shadow Priest in-group for mana. Polymorph, Slow, Counterspell.
- **Affliction Warlock** — DoTs + **Curse of the Elements** (+10%, or **+13% with Malediction** — magic damage taken; the caster-group reason to bring one). Other curses: Recklessness, Tongues, Doom. *CC:* Fear, Howl of Terror, Banish, Seduction.
- **Demonology Warlock** — Felguard-pet spec, strong survivability. **No raid spell-power aura in TBC** (Demonic Pact is Wrath); brings curses + Imp's Blood Pact (stamina). Same lock CC.
- **Destruction Warlock** — highest lock DPS; Shadow Bolt spam + Ruin. **Improved Shadow Bolt** (+20% shadow damage taken on the target). Curses + Shadowfury (stun).
- **Balance Druid (Moonkin)** — Starfire + Moonfire/Insect Swarm DoTs; **Moonkin Aura** (+3% spell crit, party). **Improved Faerie Fire = +melee/ranged hit in TBC** (a physical-DPS debuff). Insect Swarm (−2% target hit). *CC:* Cyclone, Hibernate, Roots; Innervate, Rebirth.
- **Elemental Shaman** — premier caster-group support; Lightning Bolt + **Totem of Wrath** (+3% spell crit & hit) + **Wrath of Air Totem** (+spell power) + **Bloodlust/Heroism**. *CC:* none hard (Grounding/Tremor totems, Earth Shock interrupt, Purge).

**The one design lesson for breach "classes":** TBC value came from **irreplaceable, *non-overlapping* team effects** — a debuff that multiplies the whole group (Misery, Curse of Elements, Expose Weakness, Blood Frenzy), a unique utility (battle-res, Bloodlust, Mana Tide, Innervate), a shared-but-exclusive slot (armor debuff). That's what made a comp feel like a puzzle rather than a stat-stack.

## 3. Shotgun spread + classic SWAT weapons

### Real 00 buckshot spread (your instinct was right — games exaggerate it badly)
- The folk rule **"~1 inch of spread per yard"** *overestimates reality by ~40–50%*. Measured cylinder / improved-cylinder smoothbores pattern at **~0.6 in/yard** (≈ 1.6 cm per meter). Use that for a believable model.
- **Pattern diameter (realistic band):** 5 m ≈ 8 cm · 10 m ≈ **16–17 cm** · 15 m ≈ 25 cm · 25 m ≈ 42 cm.
- A man is ~50 cm wide. **At 10 m the pattern is only ~⅓ of the target → all 8–9 pellets land**, with margin. Pellets only start missing a man past **~18–30 m** — which is coincidentally about where buckshot stops being reliably effective anyway.
- **Game model:** cone **half-angle ~0.5°** (realistic), up to ~1° if you want the loose "1 in/yard" feel. Not the 15–30° cones games usually use that turn the shotgun into a miss-machine past 5 m.
- **00 buck = 9 pellets** standard (8 is common and tighter), each ~0.33 in / **8.4 mm**.

### Classic CQB / SWAT long guns (beyond the shotgun)
- **H&K MP5** — *yes, the one you guessed.* 9mm, roller-delayed, low recoil, very accurate; **the** iconic tactical SMG (cemented by the 1980 SAS Iranian Embassy siege). Variants worth modeling: **A2/A3** (fixed / sliding stock — standard SWAT), **K** ("Kurz," chopped/concealable), **SD** (integrally suppressed — quiet with standard ammo).
- **Benelli M4 / M1014** — semi-auto 12-ga; the modern **tactical + door-breaching shotgun** (USMC + police). Pair it with the spread numbers above.
- **Colt M4 / CAR-15** — 5.56 short-barrel carbine; the default modern **entry/patrol rifle** for SWAT teams.
- **Modern additions if you want them:** H&K **UMP** (.45-capable MP5 successor), H&K **MP7** (compact PDW), FN **P90** (5.7mm bullpup, unmistakable silhouette).
