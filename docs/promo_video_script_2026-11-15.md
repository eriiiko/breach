# Promo video: script and shot list (draft 1)

2026-11-15. Written with Erik in one planning session; this is the first full
pass and everything in it is Erik's to change. Tracker: #207, milestone 24
(Presentation).

The video is the film of Mission 1, "Silent Cargo"
(`docs/missions/missions.md`), shortened to about three and a half minutes.
Every shot below names what it shows off and what must exist for it, so this
document is also the build list.

## 1. What changed against the written mission

The mission text is from before today's session. Where they differ, this
script follows what Erik said on 2026-11-15:

| Mission text | This script |
|---|---|
| A squad of marines | Three autonomous government agents: Beatrice, the player character, a third man |
| One zombie seen in a flashlight on the way in | Nothing alive is seen until the cockpit; the dark half is nearly silent |
| The bridge is empty; the kill team attacks the squad | The agents are attacked at the cockpit door, shut themselves in, and watch the second party on the cameras |
| The Gray took a case from the bridge | The Gray is in a container in the cargo bay (never seen); the second party finds it and dies |
| Everyone gets out | The third agent is bitten: the start of the game's story (stasis, the hunt for an antidote) |
| The ship sinks | The ship is in space; the charge's timer reaches zero and the picture cuts to black |

Still open from the mission text: the ship's name (*MV Caduceus* there) and
which organisation owns it (Erik wondered about something other than UNHCR).
The script says "aid ship" and shows no logo yet.

## 2. The people

- **Beatrice.** Leads without saying so. Fast, exact, kind in one sentence
  and then back to the task.
- **The player character.** Working name **Cole** (a placeholder so the
  dialogue reads; Erik names him or her). Carries the shotgun and the med-kit.
- **The third agent.** Working name **Marsh** (placeholder). The one who is
  bitten.
- **The second party.** Six in heavy armour. No insignia, no voices.
- **The crew.** All turned.
- **The Gray.** Never on screen. A container, a blue light, what it leaves.

The agents wear sealed suits with helmets, because they leave through a hole
in the hull at the end.

## 3. The script

Times are a target for a cut of about 3:30. "Shows" is the game system the
shot is there to show off.

### Scene 1: the cargo bay (0:00 to 0:35)

Black. White text, small: *Aid vessel. No contact for three days.*

A loading door opens on darkness. Three flashlight beams come in and
separate. The bay is big: stacked crates with aid markings, pallets, shelving,
one flashing orange warning lamp that lights the room for a moment each turn
and leaves it black in between.

A beam crosses a container that does not belong: unmarked, a different
material, frost on its edges, a thin cold mist creeping from under it.
Beatrice's light stays on it one second longer than the others. Nobody says
anything. They move on.

### Scene 2: the dark ship (0:35 to 1:15)

Corridors and crew quarters, seen from above. The picture is black except
where a beam falls. A mess room: a meal half eaten, a chair on its side. A
sleeping cabin: a bunk, a locker open.

A corridor with white walls. A beam slides along the wall and stops on a
splash of blood. The white around it turns faintly red, and so does the
agent's sleeve. Beatrice raises a hand: seen, keep moving.

As the last beam swings away from a side room, its edge passes for a few
frames over someone standing still in the dark, facing the wall. The agents
do not see it. The viewer does.

### Scene 4: the cockpit (1:40 to 2:20)

Beatrice finds the main lights. Through the ship they come on, section by

### Scene 5: the second boarding (2:20 to 2:55)

One monitor shows the cargo bay door opening again. Six figures in heavy
armour come in and spread out like people who have done this before.

The film leaves the monitors and shows it at full size: the heavies meet the
crew in the corridors. Rifle fire, a grenade through a doorway, the blast
rolling out of the room, fire catching, smoke filling the corridor and pouring
through the next door when it opens. A pipe bursts; water spreads over the
floor and the fire shines in it. They do not slow down. Back on the monitor:
one of them kneels and fixes a charge to a bulkhead. Digits light up and
start to count.

Then, on the cargo bay camera, two of them stop at the odd container. They
signal the others. All six gather. One reaches for it.

Blue-white. The cargo camera goes to static. On the other feeds, blue light
pulses out of the cargo bay doorway, four times, five. Then every feed is
still.

### Scene 6: out (2:55 to 3:25)

The cockpit door opens. They run, Marsh between the other two. The ship is
lit now and they pass everything the dark hid: the blood, the bodies, the
burnt room still smoking, the water on the floor. They pass the charge. Its
digits are well under a minute.

The cargo bay. The heavies are there, in pieces, the floor and walls marked
in a way no weapon they carried could mark them. The container is open and
empty, cold mist rolling out of it. Behind it the hull is torn open: stars,
and the air of the whole ship going out through the hole, taking the loose
things with it.

They do not stop to look. Helmets sealed, they go out through the hole.

## 5. What is true and never said

Kept from the lore (`docs/lore_the_grays.md`, the mission text) and from
Claude's proposal of 2026-11-15, which Erik has not yet confirmed:

- The medicine in the hold carried the Grays' agent. The crew handled a
  broken crate and turned within two days.
- A Gray came aboard to take back what was left and was still there,
  sheltering in the container, when the agents arrived. It never meant them
  harm and never meant them good.
- The second party was sent to erase the ship by people who believe the
  outbreak is their own failure.
- The agents' report will be buried. Marsh goes into stasis. The first
  missions of the game are the hunt for an antidote, against his clock.
