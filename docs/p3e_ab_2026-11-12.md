# P3e A/B — the match path to integer, before vs after (2026-11-12)

#29 P3e (`docs/residency_batching_design_2026-11-07.md` §7, the re-inventoried
site table) made every float site of the continuous match path integer: the
shot cone and the rolls draw integers (`simulation/draws.py`), the march, the
mitigation and the hp are Q16, the blast falloff, the payload deposits and
FieldEdit are integer, and slot 9c's heat row and 9c3's poison dose are
Q16 rationals. This changes the game a little — different random values,
different rounding — so it was measured before and after in real units.

**Before** = main `24b87481` (a detached checkout with the same compiled
module). **After** = branch `29-p3e-integer-match` at `ce5bee2f` (the code of
every group; the later commits move pins and docs only, bar a rename).
s.e. = standard error of the mean over matches; "diff / s.e." is the
difference over the combined s.e. (|x| < 2 is inside seed noise).

`tools/bot_tournament.py --levels ship_demo --seeds 1-72 --a bots --b bots --zombies 0`

Rounds per kill 9.3 → 8.7; hits per round 0.86 → 0.91. Marine deaths by
cause: bullet 310 → 295, heat 114 → 138, explosion 17 → 20.

**Findings, stated plainly.** (a) On seeds 1–24 alone the rounds fired per
team moved −2.3 s.e. (28.2 → 24.2); with 48 more seeds the difference is
−1.2 s.e. — the first reading was seed noise. (b) Heat deaths in the
firefight rose 114 → 138 (+21 %, about 1.6 s.e. on a Poisson count). The
blast scenes below show the same heat damage to the hundredth of an HP per
unit, so this is most likely which matches burned (the frags light the
furniture), not a stronger heat row — but it is the number to watch at the
play test.

## 3. Focus fire (`tools/bench_focus_fire.py`)

IDENTICAL before and after, every case (zombie / marine, continuous /
single, 1–4 shooters): e.g. one shooter needs 34 rounds / 4.71 s on a
zombie and 8 rounds / 0.625 s on a marine, four 24 / 0.458 s and
8 / 0.125 s. At the bench distance every round of the 3° cone lands on the
3x3 target, so the new cone draws do not change a hit, and the integer
mitigation is exact for the carbine's integer packets.

## 4. Blasts in real units

A scratch driver (the four `levels/blast_final` timed charges — frag 1,
breach 0.1, magazine cook-off 8, C4 12 — with 40 marines at 3–12 tiles east
and west of them, 3.2 s; and a frag thrown down a 5-wide 0.333 m corridor
at marines 14–36 tiles from the thrower, 3 s).

- **Walls destroyed**: 0 and 0 in both.
- **Damage per unit**: every one of the 48 units within 0.25 HP of before,
  most within 0.01 (frag at 3 tiles 12.579 → 12.588, cook-off at 5 tiles
  100.24 → 100.03, C4 at 12 tiles 78.52 → 78.58). Blast (direct) damage is
  identical (538 HP total, `falloff_floor` is exactly `int(max * falloff)`);
  the heat damage 3411.20 → 3411.22 HP.
- **Knocked down / dead**: the same units, every one.
- **Fields**: the summed temperature 2.6226e10 → 2.6218e10 raw (−0.03 %),
  the summed smoke 61 373 478 → 61 373 561 counts (+0.0001 %).
- **Corridor frag**: the same two dead (c20, c23), c17 15.789 → 15.780,
  c26 28.70 → 28.77 HP.

## 5. Verdict

Every difference is inside seed noise or below a tenth of an HP; nothing
needs a ruling. The two numbers to watch at the play test are the
firefight's heat deaths (§2 (b)) and the feel of the shot cone (the same
±3°, new random values).
