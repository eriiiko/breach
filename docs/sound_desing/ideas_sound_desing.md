# Sound design — ideas

Append-only capture of sound design ideas. Not canon, not scheduled — a
parking lot until a proper arc picks these up.

## Idea 1 — ambient radios and TVs as world-building (2026-09-30)

The ship's ambient sound shouldn't just be hums and hisses — it should
include radios playing music and TVs delivering news, like a normal
inhabited space. References to normal life (music, news, sports) make the
atmosphere more realistic and grounded before things go wrong.

- TVs could show static, muted sports/news, or unmuted broadcasts —
  on/off/muted is a per-TV state, not a fixed rule.
- Whatever people were watching/listening to should generally be *on* —
  depending on the setting (a break room TV left running, a bridge radio
  tuned to a station, a dead room with only static).
- News broadcasts are a natural vehicle for telling story/lore
  incidentally — the player picks up world context by walking past a TV,
  not through exposition.

### Open question — music rights

We want radio music playing in the game but have no rights to real music.
Options to explore:
- Generate original, generic pop-style tracks per genre (procedural or
  commissioned) to loop as "radio stations."
- Stream from Spotify or similar — unclear licensing/feasibility for a
  game context, needs research.
- Let artists opt in to have their music featured in the game (a
  promotional slot) — could become a neat community feature, but is a
  business/licensing conversation, not an engineering one.

No decision made — this needs a licensing/legal pass before any
implementation direction is chosen.
